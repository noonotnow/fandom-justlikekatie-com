---
name: Fan report usability
description: Creator feedback and the usability boundary for reporting a public image.
---

Fan image reporting should be available from the ordinary image preview, not
require entering full-screen mode. Keep it collapsible and ensure expanded
controls remain readable and reachable on small or short screens.

**Why:** During live verification, the creator said the full-screen-only form was
"almost impossible to read and interact with" and that the report options could
not really be seen. This prevented verification from proceeding.

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing visual identity, but treat report
readability as a functional requirement. Test expanded controls with long source
titles and portrait/landscape viewports, not just the collapsed report heading.
