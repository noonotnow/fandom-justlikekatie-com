---
name: Soundtrack source boundary
description: Fan OST lists are discovery leads, not proof of licensed music release or territorial availability.
---

Use fan-maintained soundtrack lists to identify search terms, but confirm release dates, track roles, artists and catalog size against a licensed music service or label. A fan list of numbered vocal parts can disagree with a full licensed album containing instrumentals; avoid merging those counts.

**Why:** The Against the Current fan OST list and Apple Music catalog disagreed on album date and count. The music storefront supplied direct track-level credits and country-specific album listings, while neither source proved subscription playback.

**How to apply:** When publishing a music guide, cite only verified service/label detail pages for asserted credits, name each checked storefront as a listing rather than a playback guarantee, and keep potentially spoiler-bearing later track names out of public previews.
