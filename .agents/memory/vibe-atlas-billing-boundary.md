---
name: Vibe Atlas billing boundary
description: Product and architecture boundary for the first paid Vibe Atlas membership.
---

The first paid offer is one monthly Vibe Atlas Collector subscription. Verified Stripe subscription state is the source of paid entitlement. Free users can browse current and historical editorial records, click into the active Actor of the Day, and, during the active Daily Drop window, use the complete Star of the Day inventory in the homepage builder, download individual images, save selected images to My Collection, and complete, rearrange, export, and share a standard 3×3 grid. The separate My Collection builder uses only explicit user saves. A signed-in free account can sync explicitly saved Collection items across devices after choosing to merge; sign-in never grants the complete pack catalog. Collector gates reopening missed historical inventories in their own builder context, reconstructing or remixing prior days, acquiring missed historical images, and generating full grids from approved released packs. Saving those individual images or complete grids to My Collection is an explicit Collector action, separate from automatically retained private run history. Collector also gates additional saved canvases, approved premium treatments, and eligible Master Exports.

PostgreSQL is limited to Stripe-synchronized billing records and the minimal account-to-customer link. Existing authentication, IndexedDB, Blob Collections, packets, content storage, and MemeForge behavior stay on their current systems. MemeForge remains free and shareable.

**Why:** The broader CREATE multi-tenant blueprint carried substantial authorization, transaction, publishing, and deployment risk. The creator clarified that free account sync and saved Collection ownership are distinct from paid access to the full runnable catalog and historical utility.

**How to apply:** Require account authentication, account-scoped ownership, and device-merge consent for bulk free Collection sync; an explicit single-item save may sync without merging unrelated local saves. Keep full pack browsing and fresh full-grid generation behind synchronized Stripe capability checks on the server. Public contrast previews remain view-only. Use hosted Checkout and Customer Portal, do not migrate unrelated product data into PostgreSQL, and keep Creator OS handoff exclusive to Creator OS or an ecosystem bundle.
