---
name: Vibe Atlas billing boundary
description: Product and architecture boundary for the first paid Vibe Atlas membership.
---

The first paid offer is one monthly Vibe Atlas Collector subscription. Verified Stripe subscription state is the source of paid entitlement. Free users can browse current and historical editorial records, click into the active Actor of the Day, and, during the active Daily Drop window, use the complete Star of the Day inventory in the homepage builder, download individual images, save selected images to My Collection, and complete, rearrange, export, and share a standard 3×3 grid. The separate My Collection builder uses only explicit user saves. A free signed-in account may sync that explicitly saved Collection across devices after choosing to merge this device; authentication is not a Collector entitlement. Collector gates reopening missed historical inventories in their own builder context, reconstructing or remixing prior days, acquiring missed historical images, and running full grids from the approved released-pack catalog, plus saved premium runs, additional canvases, approved treatments, and eligible Master Exports. Saving an image or complete Collector grid is a deliberate My Collection action, not a consequence of retained private run history.

PostgreSQL is limited to Stripe-synchronized billing records and the minimal account-to-customer link. Existing authentication, IndexedDB, Blob Collections, packets, content storage, and MemeForge behavior stay on their current systems. MemeForge remains free and shareable.

**Why:** The broader CREATE multi-tenant blueprint carried substantial authorization, transaction, publishing, and deployment risk. The creator clarified that free account sync and saved Collection ownership are distinct from paid access to the complete runnable pack catalog and historical utility; login must not accidentally grant Collector access.

**How to apply:** Require account authentication, account-scoped ownership, and device-merge consent for bulk free Collection sync, but do not require a paid Collector capability for it. An explicit single-item save may sync without merging unrelated local items. Continue deriving full released-pack browsing, live nine-card generation, saved premium runs, and historical reconstruction permissions server-side from synchronized Stripe state; a free login must fail those protected checks. Keep public teasers view-only and editorial Archive records distinct from gated historical creation tools. Use hosted Checkout and Customer Portal, do not migrate unrelated product data into PostgreSQL, and keep Creator OS handoff exclusive to Creator OS or an ecosystem bundle.

**Implementation caution:** Collection sync owns explicitly saved account-scoped records, not the ability to create protected historical grids or use premium exports. Keep paid creation and export entitlement checks on their own routes; never infer paid capability from a synced saved record. Existing operator checks on non-C-drama meme records are a legacy isolation safeguard, not a paid-product distinction or roadmap signal.
