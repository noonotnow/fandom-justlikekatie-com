# Xianxia decoder joke engine

**Rule:** Decoder cards pair what a newcomer sees with what a veteran knows. The veteran line must reveal why the romance or narrative machinery absurdly requires the event; merely renaming the trope or predicting its outcome is not enough.

**Why:** The humor comes from experienced viewers recognizing the deranged plot necessity behind the danger. “Certain death” is funny when the veteran diagnosis is that amnesia is required to advance the enemies-to-lovers arc—not simply because cliff falls often produce amnesia.

**How to apply:** Review every veteran line for a specific romantic, emotional, or structural necessity. Keep launch filters dense: use three or four broad categories for roughly twelve to fourteen cards, and only expand toward seven categories after the decoder has about eighteen to twenty-four cards.
