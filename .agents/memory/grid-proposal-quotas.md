---
name: Grid proposal quotas
description: Future access boundary for proposing multiple generated grid alternatives.
---

Vibe Atlas may later propose more than one grid from the available collection. Free accounts can receive a configurable number of successful proposal generations per day; Collector access may receive a higher allowance or a different policy.

**Why:** Multiple proposals add useful computational and editorial depth without weakening the complete free creation loop. A daily allowance gives free users a real taste of the feature while keeping a meaningful membership benefit.

**How to apply:** Count successful proposal-generation actions, not manual rearrangements, edits, shares, or exports. Individual-image saves are governed separately by Collector access. Do not consume quota for server failures or invalid responses. Keep the numeric allowance configurable and do not hardcode it into public copy until usage data supports the final limit.
