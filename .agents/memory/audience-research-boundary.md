---
name: Audience research boundary
description: Evidence standard for episode-specific Reddit reactions and hot-search insights in bounded editorial articles.
---

Use readable, episode-checked discussion excerpts for audience attributions. Search results mentioning a thread in a subreddit sidebar do not establish its comments, date, or permalink. Ordinary search placement and promotional heat metrics do not establish historical hot-search rank.

**Why:** Audience research for a bounded installment returned unrelated dramas, aggregated sidebar mentions, out-of-range topics, and later-episode contamination. The public Reddit read route was access-blocked, so search fragments could not verify the proposed reaction claims.

**How to apply:** Record source access limits, keep unverified reactions and trend claims out of the article, and request readable excerpts when required audience research cannot otherwise be completed. Distinguish host recap from comments and check later-spoiler contamination even in a correctly titled thread.

For Vibing Now copy, let scenes carry the argument. Reddit conversations may frame the analytical direction, but remove Reddit-reporting passages and keep the editorial process out of published copy. Crediting a thread is welcome; make the attribution subtle rather than repeatedly reporting what commenters said. Shelf framing should let readers choose by last episode watched, not presume a shared platform schedule.

**Why:** The creator said direct thread references read like a news report from the internet, and explicitly requested this cleanup for the three episode-bounded readings.

**How to apply:** Preserve useful analytical questions, scene analysis, humor, and each article's episode boundary. Keep source credit separate from the argument. This copy-edit direction is not new factual certification or permission to add unverified availability dates.

Keep the private reading separate from its audit. Establish the coercion once, develop the episode-specific change in understanding, then let the emotional complication carry the ending rather than qualifying each affectionate moment.

**Why:** The creator agreed to—and enthusiastically confirmed the result of—a prose-focused pass that lets tenderness breathe, builds the intervening scenes toward the burial reveal, retains the humor, and separates the reading from the long review appendix.

**How to apply:** Provide a short, complete reading without review-process passages. Maintain the claim register separately; neither supported interpretations nor rewatch observations are inherently suspect. Editorial direction is not exact-copy or publication approval.
