---
name: Vibing Now episode boundaries
description: Editorial review principle for publishing current-state drama readings without next-episode leaks.
---

An editor-approved article can still contain a plot event from beyond its advertised episode boundary. Check each consequential plot claim against episode-specific watch notes before publication, and obtain approval for any substantive removal or reassignment; a deploy preview alone is never source review.

**Why:** A draft marked ready for an Episodes 22–25 reading included a Zheng-family sentencing sequence placed in Episode 26 by the creator's watch notes. The creator approved removing that section from the earlier reading because the later article already addressed it.

**How to apply:** For each future Vibing Now installment, compare plot claims with the creator's watched/source material and declared endpoint, record the review date, and keep later-episode details out of earlier article bodies and discussions. Do not treat a spoiler-boundary disclaimer as proof the body is safe.
