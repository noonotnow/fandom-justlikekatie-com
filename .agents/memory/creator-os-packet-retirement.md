---
name: Creator OS packet retirement
description: Product boundary for replacing Idea Packets without losing trusted provenance or history.
---

Idea Packets are no longer the operator-facing creation model. New grid actions should say “Make a post in Creator OS,” while packet records remain an internal compatibility and archive layer.

**Why:** Packets still provide trusted provenance, version checks, render manifests, receipts, and historical Middle-earth compatibility. Deleting or bypassing them before Creator OS accepts the replacement contract would weaken safety and break old records.

**How to apply:** New creator workflows should use the versioned Creator Draft source concept. Keep historical packets readable. Stop creating compatibility packets only after Creator OS accepts ordered grid/image sources directly with equivalent authentication, idempotency, and trusted-media validation.
