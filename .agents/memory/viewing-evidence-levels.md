---
name: Viewing evidence levels
description: Accept narrowly attributed firsthand viewing and supplied calendars without inventing territorial rights
---

Do not collapse “not independently verified” into “no usable evidence.” A direct viewer confirmation can support a dated, country- and tier-specific episode/subtitle observation. A supplied branded calendar can support an attributed transcription of planned dates, with unauthenticated provenance made explicit.

**Why:** Requiring independent country-local browsing for every detail discarded useful firsthand Express playback information and schedule imagery. It also confused the release schedule question with the territorial-access question.

**How to apply:** Ask only for missing country, tier, episode and subtitle facts. Keep firsthand observations, calendar transcriptions and provider listing checks distinct. Never infer a full episode range, a finale, another country’s rights or equivalence between Tencent VIP/SVIP and WeTV Express. New observations must not refresh older evidence dates.

For editorial episode readings, accept the creator's explicit confirmation of onscreen dialogue as attributed rewatch evidence. Do not keep labelling it speculation or repeatedly asking for the same confirmation merely because independent playback is unavailable.

**Why:** Repeatedly treating a creator-confirmed courtroom rule as inference caused unnecessary clarification requests and kept an already answered factual question marked as a blocker.

**How to apply:** Distinguish reported dialogue from the creator's interpretation and from independent subtitle verification. Record the confirmation, update stale blockers, and paraphrase within the drama's scope; do not invent exact quotations, timestamps, or general historical law. Final-copy and release approval remain separate.
