---
name: Star of the Day collection loop
description: Public archive building, calendar-age acquisition, and deliberate Collection source isolation.
---

The Star of the Day collection is a server-owned published edition inventory for the public, nonmember edition. It is not displayed or synchronized wholesale as part of My Collection. While an edition is the active Daily Drop, everyone can use its complete inventory in the free grid builder, download any individual image, save selected images to My Collection, and export or share the resulting standard grid. Current and historical Daily Drop editorial records remain publicly browsable.

Everyone can build and standard-export grids from available verified published Star of the Day archive images, regardless of edition age. Only individual acquisition through official app save actions is Collector-gated after three calendar days. Interpret age using authoritative Asia/Shanghai edition dates: today and days one through three are free; day four and older require verified Collector entitlement. Missing publication days must not extend this window. This explicitly supersedes the earlier paid-only historical-building boundary for the public archive source. Deeper released actor packs and premium tools remain paid.

**Why:** The creator explicitly confirmed that older archived images should be available for everyone's grid creation, while acquisition of individual older images remains the membership distinction. Publicly delivered image bytes cannot be protected against browser-level copying.

**How to apply:** Keep public composition/export, individual card acquisition, and finished-grid saves separate. Enforce the age and verified membership check before a new official individual save, not before grid proposals or standard exports. Never auto-save constituent cards. Existing legitimately saved images remain usable after aging or membership expiry, and finished-grid saves retain their canvas limits.

Every actor with a released pack should have a permanent, indexable, always-free actor page that introduces the actor and every released pack with a substantive visual teaser and limited provenance. Articles should use these stable public destinations, not a membership gate or an active-day URL. The active Actor of the Day spotlights the selected nine-card Daily Drop in full for everyone and promotes the actor's other released packs through their free teasers; it does not unlock every full pack. Collector membership unlocks complete fresh grids from any approved released actor × vibe pairing at any time, plus saving and reopening generated runs. Unreleased candidates remain editorial mentions, not released packs. This member browsing exposes curated pack material, not raw searches, diagnostics, rejected results, or unpublished candidates.

The public archive builder and My Collection builder are separate source contexts. The public builder uses verified published Star of the Day inventory across dates and supports actor filtering, composition, standard export, and explicit finished-grid saves. The My Collection builder uses only images the user explicitly saved; it must never inject Daily Drop inventory, historical editions, actor-pack suggestions, or other unsaved images. Historical edition or actor-pack views may offer explicit Save actions or launch their own clearly labeled builder context, but they do not silently expand My Collection.

This separation is user-confirmed: the homepage is where someone discovers a strong image they do not yet have, while the My Collection builder must feel entirely authored from their own deliberate saves.

**Why:** A useful daily collection gives free visitors a complete creation loop and a reason to return. Stable visual teasers let articles and search visitors discover the actor's catalog without a surprise paywall, while paid archive utility and fresh grids from other released packs offer deeper Collector value. Star of the Day is a public spotlight, not a restriction on which released actors Collectors can choose.

**How to apply:** Keep every builder's source boundary explicit. Permanent teaser pages should be useful even when the actor is not today's star, with real approved visuals where available; never imply an unpublished pairing or an unavailable preview is released. Public archive images must come from verified immutable publications, not private searches, preview runs, or unverified catalog rows. Preserve edition provenance independently of original source attribution through grids, exports, and publishing handoffs. A legacy full-board gate must not prevent public archive building or reaching individual-save actions.

An active free nine-card drop can exist without a qualifying historical public pack record. Its actor/vibe identity alone must not create a signed-out historical-record deep link. The actor's other approved, runnable packs should instead offer bounded search-result contrast previews alongside today's free full grid; those preview runs do not change the published Daily Drop's fixed composition.

**Why:** A homepage handoff once pointed signed-out visitors at the day's actor/vibe preview even though the free grid existed but no qualifying public teaser did, producing a misleading “preview not found” error.

**How to apply:** Keep active-day grid access, runnable pack eligibility, preview runs, and historical publication records as separate concepts. Do not use transient source URLs as durable historical image delivery or silently promote a preview run into a permanent pack board. The current daily pack is already free in full; show three-to-six safe cards from each *other* eligible pack for that actor, while the Collector can run the full fresh grid. Historical edition manifests remain immutable.
