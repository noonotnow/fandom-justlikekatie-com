---
name: Star of the Day collection loop
description: Free daily acquisition, public archive browsing, and Collector remix access for Vibe Atlas grids.
---

The Star of the Day collection is a server-owned published edition inventory for the public, nonmember edition. It is not displayed or synchronized as part of My Collection. Everyone can build a standard grid with the current inventory and keep using individual cards they explicitly saved while those cards were freely available. Current and historical Daily Drop editorial records remain publicly browsable.

Collectors can reach back into prior Star of the Day collections to add material they missed and build new grids. Deeper actor packs add broader member-only choices, so Collector collections and compositions naturally diverge from the canonical Daily Drops rather than simply reproducing them.

**Why:** A useful daily collection gives free visitors a complete creation loop and a reason to return. Paid archive utility solves the understandable “I missed that drop” problem, while deeper actor packs create expressive value and collection diversity without paywalling the public editorial record.

**How to apply:** Separate public historical browsing from card acquisition and reuse rights. A Daily Drop or archive page may reveal the edition inventory, but only an explicit Save action materializes one selected card in My Collection. Never bulk-copy an edition or show the same assets as both inventory and user saves. Gate importing missed historical cards and deeper actor-pack material, not the record page itself. Never remove a card from a free user’s local collection merely because its public acquisition day ended. Preserve provenance from Daily Drop or actor pack through saves, grids, exports, and publishing handoffs.
