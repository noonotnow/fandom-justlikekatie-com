name: Actor image feedback
description: Editorial boundary for image-level picks and rejections in private actor preflight reviews.
---

**Rule:** Image feedback is append-only and bound to the frozen audit run. Pin, hero, and supporting choices are weak preferences for the next review, not overrides of the current result. Composite, unusable, failed-load, and hard anti-anchor images remain blocked. Exact source copies share one image identity and resolve to the canonical retained representative.

**Why:** Operators need to recover strong images the automated board omitted without turning subjective feedback into a bypass around identity, safety, promise, or duplicate gates.

**How to apply:** Require the blind Event/Compiled/Neither choice first. Build rescue arrangements only from frozen evidence, treat changed-feedback arrangements as historical, and let prior picks break close ties only after every current qualification still passes.
