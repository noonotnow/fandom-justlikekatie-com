name: Editorial record and collectible utility boundary
description: Public authority, protected product data, and the experiential premium model for Vibe Atlas.
---

This is the user-confirmed product doctrine for Vibe Atlas.

Index the editorial record; gate the collectible utility. Current and historical actor and edition records remain publicly browsable. Their pages should contain substantive approved context, standard-resolution previews, canonical entity relationships, publication dates, and source credits where appropriate. Raw retrieval inventories, search spells, diagnostics, rejected media, internal scores, unpublished combinations, complete boards where gated, and complete pack payloads stay private.

**Why:** Authority comes from transformation—retrieval, judgment, classification, context, and a durable artifact—not from publishing scraped-looking result inventories. A useful public record supports discovery without leaking the product or creating thin, duplicative pages.

**How to apply:** Use explicit public DTOs rather than masked versions of private payloads. Give actor/vibe combinations indexable pages only when they have an approved editorial identity, approved copy, a valid sample, related context, and an intentional canonical. Treat Notion as the editorial copy source when that publishing flow is active; code/config fallback copy is not the long-term authority. Use stable archive paths, materialized publication OG images, and shared caching only for responses guaranteed identical for everyone.

Premium should emerge from artifact quality and utility, not repeated lock messaging. Vibe Atlas Canvas is the fandom creation surface; Creator OS Workstation is the production and publishing surface. Never claim export dimensions, color spaces, or source-file rights the delivered asset does not actually provide.

**Why:** A genuinely useful free creation loop builds desire and trust. Paid value is safer and stronger when it adds depth, persistence, treatments, eligible higher-resolution composites, and provenance-preserving workflow rather than unrestricted third-party source files.

**How to apply:** Let free users finish and share a standard grid. Keep attribution obligations intact at every tier. Reserve “Send to Creator OS” for a real cross-product transfer, and keep Collector and Creator OS as separate capabilities.
