---
name: Archive public links
description: When to advertise permanent public record links from dated Archive entries.
---

**Rule:** A saved Archive entry's public-record paths are historical metadata, not proof that the linked public pages can be served. Advertise a permanent actor/edition record only when its immutable publication manifest still passes the public editorial and media checks; otherwise keep the dated Archive entry navigable without a record link.

**Why:** The Archive listed permanent links for many older editions whose public pages returned “Public record not found.” The public record service checks current manifest indexability, while the Archive catalogue can retain old link metadata even when that check fails.

**How to apply:** Validate links at the Archive read boundary against the same public-record rules, fail closed if verification is unavailable, and preserve the dated Archive route as the fallback. Repair older public records separately with verified source assets and substantive editorial copy; do not relax the public allowlist just to make links resolve.
