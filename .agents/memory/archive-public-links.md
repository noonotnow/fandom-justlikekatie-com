name: Archive public links
description: When to advertise permanent public record links from dated Archive entries.
---

**Rule:** A saved Archive entry's public-record paths are historical metadata, not proof that the linked public pages can be served. Advertise a permanent actor/edition record only when its immutable publication manifest still passes the public editorial and media checks; otherwise keep the dated Archive entry navigable without a record link.

**Why:** The Archive listed permanent links for many older editions whose public pages returned “Public record not found.” The public record service checks current manifest indexability, while the Archive catalogue can retain old link metadata even when that check fails.

**How to apply:** Validate links at the Archive read boundary against the same public-record rules, fail closed if verification is unavailable, and preserve the dated Archive route as the fallback. Repair older public records separately with verified source assets and substantive editorial copy; do not relax the public allowlist just to make links resolve.

The creator confirmed that the existing approved Chinese/English Vibe Pack name and accompanying lines are sufficient editorial copy for a public edition; do not ask them to draft or re-approve a longer English paragraph solely to pass a character threshold. This does not waive the separate requirement for a dated publication manifest and verification of all nine immutable MEDIA deliveries.

**Why:** The Archive can show an approved bilingual pack from its own dated record even when no immutable publication manifest exists, and a blanket change to a copy-length predicate could expose unverified manifests.

**How to apply:** Treat the approved bilingual pair as editorial sign-off, but release a specific dated manifest only after its exact nine image deliveries have been independently verified. Dates with no manifest stay Archive-only; never reconstruct public records from Builder or Collection data.

For historical copy approvals, show the exact original Chinese and English
names and lines directly in chat in manageable dated batches; do not rely on
a downloadable review file alone.

**Why:** The creator could not view the standalone review file meaningfully.
They completed the date-specific review using the copy shown directly in chat.

**How to apply:** Keep each approval tied to the date and exact displayed copy.
File-based audit evidence may supplement, but must not replace, readable
in-chat editorial review.
