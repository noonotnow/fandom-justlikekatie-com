---
name: Released pairing semantics
description: Distinguishes approval of a Vibe Atlas actor/vibe search recipe from approval of one nine-image publication.
---

**Rule:** A currently approved actor × vibe pairing has graduated its search depth and release definition. Both ordinary approvals with two release confirmations and explicit approved overrides count as released, including overrides before both confirmations. If it qualifies for the public Daily Drop scheduler, Collectors should be able to open it as a pack and generate a fresh visual grid from its approved search rules. Approval of the pairing does not mean every future result set has been individually curated, nor does it require an existing dated Daily Drop manifest. Today’s Daily Drop remains publicly accessible.

**Why:** Treating the immutable published nine-image board as the only kind of released pack left the Collector library with query-only text cards or excluded approved pairings. The creator explicitly clarified that "released" refers to the approved pairing/search definition, while fresh grids are changing outputs from that definition. The creator subsequently confirmed approved overrides count as released even before both ordinary release confirmations.

**How to apply:** Keep pack definitions and eligibility independent of individual generated grid runs. If saving generated results, retain their exact images, ordering and provenance as distinct runs rather than rewriting the approved pairing or the canonical public Daily Drop. Avoid implying fresh search results received individual human review.

**Rule:** The free Star of the Day remains one actor × one vibe × a complete shared nine-card grid. Its actor's *other* approved released packs may show bounded previews from their own runnable searches: three images initially and up to six on expansion. The active daily pairing is excluded from those contrast previews because its full nine is already free. Collector access provides full fresh nine-card grids, reruns, and saved runs for released packs. A released pack is an approved runnable actor × vibe search experience, not a pairing that has already appeared in a published Daily Drop.

**Why:** The creator corrected the fixed-manifest and all-packs-daily-preview interpretations: the discovery experience is a contrast between today's free full grid and the same actor's other vibes. Prior publication is not the test of whether a pack can run. Reusing permanent historical images as the teaser or returning an empty teaser for an eligible pack misrepresents its live search promise.

**How to apply:** For today's actor, retrieve or generate a date- and approval-bound safe sample from each other eligible pack, without exposing raw queries, diagnostics, rejected results, or the complete nine-card response to signed-out visitors. Free contrast teasers are view-only, not a way to save another pack's grid; free saves come from today's full Daily Drop. Saving and reopening non-daily generated grids require Collector. A preview run is not a new Daily Drop manifest. Keep the catalog of runnable released packs separate from immutable dated edition records, which feed the Archive and historical collection. A stable pack identity must survive label changes or array reordering; a materially new vibe/search experience should get a distinct identity rather than overwrite its predecessor. Preserve existing packs and historical records; do not implement retirement implicitly.

**Rule:** A pack teaser linked from an editorial article is contextual to that article. Making a bounded teaser free there does not add that actor or pack to the Star of the Day actor collection. An article may name an unreleased pack candidate without turning it into a runnable released pack.

**Why:** The creator distinguished the two Liu Xueyi packs discussed in an episode article from the different actor featured on the daily homepage; a global public teaser directory mixed those contexts and made Dylan Wang's view appear to contain Liu.

**How to apply:** Keep article links tied to their exact actor and vibe, and keep daily-star discovery scoped to its actor's other verified packs. Do not use article mentions as release approval, public media authority, or evidence that a pack belongs in another actor's homepage experience.

**Daily teaser decision:** The first curated grid returned for a featured day's approved pairing is the stable public teaser for that day; do not replace it with later Collector refreshes. The released-pack view should still seek fresh results and retain saved runs, rather than serving the teaser as its only grid. A permanent, indexable page additionally needs durable MEDIA-backed images and substantive editorial context; a temporary search URL or a missing copy is not enough.

**Why:** The creator explicitly prefers the first cached daily search grid as the teaser while keeping the released-pack view fresh. This separates cheap shared discovery from personal browsing without pretending each generated image was individually approved.

**How to apply:** Reuse a bounded daily snapshot for public display, preserve the original board's order, and treat later refreshed grids as separate Collector runs.
