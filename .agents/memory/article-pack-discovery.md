---
name: Article-linked pack discovery
description: Why editorial article teasers and the daily actor's pack collection must stay separate.
---

**Rule:** A bounded pack teaser linked from an editorial article is contextual to that article. Making it free there does not add the pack or actor to the Star of the Day actor collection. An article may name an unreleased candidate without making it a runnable released pack.

**Why:** The creator distinguished two Liu Xueyi packs discussed in an episode article from Dylan Wang's daily actor view; a global teaser directory mixed the contexts and implied the wrong actor belonged to today's collection.

**How to apply:** Resolve article links to exact actor/vibe pairs and scope daily-star discovery to today's actor's other verified packs. Article mentions are neither release approval nor public media authority. Never substitute another actor's teaser when the intended actor has no verified public preview.
