---
name: Daily Drop grid curation contract
description: Editorial rules for deciding between coherent Event boards and varied Compiled boards.
---

**Rule:** Treat Event as a bounded, evidence-backed exception. A coherent Event may win only when multiple provenance and visual signals support one editorial family, frame variation is sufficient, and its scored board beats a genuinely competitive Compiled alternative. Similarity alone, publisher alone, or a corroborated family with no variation must not force Event.

**Why:** The product distinguishes rhythm within one bounded editorial from search-result collapse across a compiled set. Overweighting Event produces confidently repetitive daily grids; overweighting diversity can destroy a strong visual argument.

**How to apply:** Keep fixtures in both directions: strong Event over forced variety and strong Compiled over a weak pseudo-event. Also cover publisher-only rejection, moderate similarity with corroborated provenance, extreme repetition, exact duplicates, deterministic inputs/order, identical curation receipts from both Daily Drop build paths, and cache-version separation. Receipts should expose the decisive evidence and the losing mode.
