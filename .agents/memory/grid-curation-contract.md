---
name: Daily Drop grid curation contract
description: Editorial rules for deciding between coherent Event boards and varied Compiled boards.
---

**Rule:** Treat Event as a bounded, evidence-backed exception. A coherent Event may be one appearance, scene, shoot, look, or a character mood board bounded to a specific work/role. It may win only when the boundary and frame variation are sufficient and its scored board beats a genuinely competitive Compiled alternative. Similarity alone, publisher alone, or a generic style query must not force Event.

**Why:** The product distinguishes rhythm within one bounded editorial from search-result collapse across a compiled set. Overweighting Event produces confidently repetitive daily grids; overweighting diversity can destroy a strong visual argument.

**How to apply:** Allow actor-first searches with specific work + character/role anchors to bind varied cross-publisher frames into one character Event. Keep fixtures in both directions: strong Event over forced variety and strong Compiled over a weak pseudo-event. Also cover generic-style-query rejection, publisher-only rejection, extreme repetition, exact duplicates, deterministic inputs/order, identical receipts from both Daily Drop build paths, and cache-version separation.
