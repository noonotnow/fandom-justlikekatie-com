# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Katie is the initial internal operator, collecting media signals and shaping them into reusable editorial possibilities. The packet model should remain neutral enough to support future collectors building their own themed collections.

## Product Purpose

FANDOM is a media-first collection workbench. It turns a Vibe Atlas grid and selected results into an Idea Packet that answers: “What did we collect, and what could it become?”

## Positioning

An Idea Packet sits before a Post: more structured than a loose idea, but deliberately open to becoming one carousel, several posts, lore, an explainer, a legendary or misprint entry, or a comparison.

## Operating Context

Operators start with a generated Vibe Atlas grid, curate individual media in the lightbox, refine a working angle and caption seeds, compile the media set, then hand a structured artifact to the existing CREATE/PLAN ecosystem.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Visuals are selected before copy is refined.
- A grid is the packet anchor; individual results are curated packet media.
- Media compilation is reversible and never means published or ready to publish.
- Exact duplicate media must not be added.
- Admin mutations require the existing operator authorization boundary.
- Packets persist on the server and must not be mapped one-to-one to Posts DB rows.
- Social, trading, multiplayer, and consumer account systems are out of scope.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the incumbent Vibe Atlas identity and its bilingual, gold-accented visual language. CurioSeek doctrine may inform concise product language: Collect signals → shape systems → move mountains.

## Evidence on Hand

The app contains generated daily-grid data, source result identifiers and URLs, actor and vibe metadata, an existing admin token flow, Netlify Blobs storage, and CREATE/PLAN links and handoff code.

## Product Principles

- Media first, copy second.
- Keep possibility open until the packet earns a specific output.
- Preserve provenance from collection through handoff.
- Compilation is an operational milestone, not publication.
- Reuse existing ecosystem contracts; never imply a handoff occurred when it did not.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Core curation actions must work with keyboard and touch. Reordering must include explicit non-drag controls, and loading, empty, error, duplicate, stale-media, and unauthorized states must be visible.
