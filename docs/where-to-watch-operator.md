## Manual verification and correction

1. Open each official provider listing from the **specific country** being claimed without a VPN; check the episode player and English subtitle selector as well as the listing/API. Record direct series URL, country, released episode range, free/subscription/VIP tier, subtitle completion, official source URL and exact UTC check time. A provider's general country list does not prove a particular title is licensed there. Do not generalize US access to Canada or any other country. If a provider has mixed access tiers, split it into separate records with their own episode ranges.
2. Separately inspect official original-platform, standard, advance/VIP/express and regional English-subtitle schedules if published. Record the official schedule URL, UTC check time, source time zone and finale windows. Keep absent or conflicting schedules as `unknown` or `conflict`; never infer a finale from a “full series” tile, a fan calendar, or a reported daily cadence. Preserve the previous observation in `history` with date, source and reason before changing a claim.
3. Re-review link/media rights and spoiler-safe title, cover, OG text/image, description and body; update the named reviewer and check time. `rightsReviewed` and `spoilerReviewed` must be reset to false when material changes until reviewed again. Run `npm run prepare:public`, `node --test scripts/where-to-watch.test.js scripts/public-pages.test.js` and the relevant browser check. The build fails if any registered page lacks a source review, if the checked evidence is older than seven days, or if a verified claim becomes incomplete. An `unknown` listing may remain as a qualified official link, but must not have a territory, tier, episode count or verified subtitle claim.
4. Recheck daily during active release and before finale-related copy, then at least every seven days while the page remains linked. If a check expires, conflicts or disappears, update the record and remove or qualify the claim. Withdraw the public route and hub/article links if **no** verified US option remains; rebuild the sitemap. A prior observation is history, not current proof. The visible page also marks itself overdue in the browser after seven days, but this is supplementary—not a replacement for editorial maintenance.
