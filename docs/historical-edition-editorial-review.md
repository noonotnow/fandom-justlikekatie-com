## Bounded release gate

The approval allowlist binds each of these 30 dates to its original bilingual
copy, actor/vibe identity and nine ordered MEDIA references, including asset
IDs, delivery and thumbnail URLs, checksums, sizes, MIME types, dimensions and
publication associations. Changed content fails closed even if it acquires
longer supporting copy. Existing September 3 and generic substantive-copy
behavior is unchanged.

The allowlist fingerprint has an explicitly ordered projection, so JSON
object-key ordering and unrelated public-link annotations do not invalidate
an otherwise unchanged approval. The full canonical fingerprints above
remain the independent audit evidence.

No new date is automatically approved. Missing July 31–August 2, September 28,
and October 6 manifests remain Archive-only. The audit/approval snapshot
does not certify a production release; deployment and per-page checks must
be recorded separately.
