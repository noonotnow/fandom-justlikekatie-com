# Dated viewing-guide references

Already reviewed, registered guides may remain online as dated references when their evidence ages. Expiry alone must not block unrelated site releases.

- Preserve the actual editorial, platform, region and schedule check dates. Building or rendering must never renew evidence.
- Label access, episodes, subtitles and schedules as observations at the check date. An aged guide must identify itself as a historical reference in the server-rendered HTML, including without JavaScript.
- Cached pages also qualify their notice once the oldest required evidence check expires.
- Rights/spoiler signoff, official sources, valid non-future dates, supported regional claims, qualified unknowns and conflict checks remain mandatory.
- Validation and release/spoiler advisories remain strict by default. Retaining a dated guide never establishes present availability or grants finale publication authority.

This release repairs the distinction between a dated reference and current verification. It does not establish a new review interval or settle the post-publication “Vibing Now” lifecycle.
