## Unit A — The Untamed: three names to carry into a first watch

**Format:** Public, text-only editorial board with three original typographic panels. **Reader boundary:** Pre-watch / episode 0. The board names groups appearing in the series listing but contains no episode events, relationship outcomes, character verdicts, or stills. It must not claim to certify any episode-specific safe view. The separate Field Journal remains the only episode-bounded path.

**Proposed public copy:**

> A cultivation drama can hand you many names at once. In *The Untamed*, the opening cast of groups includes Gusu Lan, Yunmeng Jiang, and Lanling Jin. Here is a no-plot way to keep three labels apart:
>
> **Gusu Lan.** Gusu is the place-name part; Lan names the family. Don't confuse Lan with *Lanling*: similar English letters do not make these the same group.
>
> **Yunmeng Jiang.** Yunmeng is the place-name part; Jiang names the family. A real historical Yunmeng landscape exists, but this fictional group's home is not a documentary map of it.
>
> **Lanling Jin.** Lanling is the place-name part; Jin names the family. You can find Lanling in present-day Shandong, but that does not place the drama's fictional world on a present-day county map.
>
> The repeatable trick is **place + family**, not “memorize every person.” Read the [place-name note](/c-drama-fandom/place-names/) for real-world context, or choose your own safe-through episode in the [Field Journal](/c-drama-fandom/watch-journal/) before loading its entries. Save a favorite card from today's Vibe Atlas drop separately if one fits; this board itself is not a Collection card.

## Unit B — When a fantasy place-name has a real-world echo

**Working Artifact:** [Place-name draft in Notion Artifacts](https://app.notion.com/p/3e88d902271f81cf94cbeb4c65daf9cb) (source draft; its Notion workflow status is separate from this website review). The Notion record carries the draft and source leads; this packet records Katie's website-publication approval. **Format:** Public, text-only cultural note related to, but not replacing, the board. **Reader boundary:** Geography and naming only; no drama plot or episode claims.

**Proposed public copy:**

> Have you heard a name in a fantasy drama, then heard it again in a different story? Not every name is invented from scratch. But a real-world echo is **not** proof that two fictional worlds share a setting—or that either story reproduces Chinese history.
>
> **Lanling (兰陵)** is a real place-name with a long history; there is a Lanling County in today's Shandong province. When *The Untamed* calls one family *Lanling Jin*, the name gives you a point of cultural recognition, not coordinates for a real sect. *Lanling* is also not the same name as *Lan*, the family name in *Gusu Lan*.
>
> **Yunmeng (云梦)** has a real geographical history too. Ancient *Yunmengze* referred to a changing wetland and lake landscape in what is now Hubei's Jianghan Plain. It silted, shifted and broke into a patchwork of lakes; research disputes the old picture of one vast, permanent lake. A modern Yunmeng County also carries the name. The *Yunmeng Jiang* family in *The Untamed* belongs to its fictional world, not to an exact map of the ancient marsh or today's county.
>
> **A first-watch lens:** When a fantasy name recurs, ask three separate questions: Is there a real place or historical name? What does this story make the name mean? And what do you *not* yet know? Keep those answers apart; you can enjoy the recognition without having to predict the plot.

**Source notes:** [Lanling County government's county overview](https://www.lanling.gov.cn/zjll.htm) locates the current county in Shandong and describes its historical name. [Hubei provincial government's lake feature](http://www.hubei.gov.cn/jmct/jcms/qhzs/index.shtml) describes the ancient Yunmeng marsh's change into Jianghan lakes; [China University of Geosciences' report on geological research](https://ses.cug.edu.cn/info/1119/11214.htm) cautions against the single-unified-lake picture. [Hubei cultural authority's Yunmeng County feature](https://wlt.hubei.gov.cn/bmdt/mtjj/202411/t20241108_5405677.shtml) identifies the modern county's relation to the old name. [Prime Video's series listing](https://www.primevideo.com/detail/0P1TY37LH4BWVS5GM2SQUHVW5G) confirms the fictional group names; do not reproduce its spoilery synopsis. Katie's [Episode 4 first-watch observation](https://app.notion.com/p/e8d71e9c6c0a4673bcf39b9699b9156e) inspired this question, but stays private. Notion architecture notes also describe a *future* place-name research idea with Lanling, Qishan, Yunmeng and Kunlun; they are not an approved article or a factual source. No borrowed map, photo, screenshot, or figure is used.
