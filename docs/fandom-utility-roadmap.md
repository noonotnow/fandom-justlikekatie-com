# FANDOM utility roadmap — decision brief

**Decision as of September 29, 2026: Freshness and trust over breadth.** Build a **trustworthy micro-catalog with measured upkeep, not a drama database**. Help a curious C-drama viewer answer **“What should I try next, and can I actually watch it where I live?”** without losing the fandom-to-Collection path. Do not launch five utilities together. Maintain and measure what is already public first; test a small editorial recommendation only if the pilot and review capacity warrant it. “Now / next / later” are decision order, **not launch dates**.

The approved companion cycle allowed roughly **12–16 editorial/rights hours weekly** for one board and one note, with daily publishing and revision still to do; the scorecard's lean case is **six hours weekly**. Neither figure is a standing commitment to maintain a catalog. Confirm a named reporting owner and actual spare editorial hours before expanding. Source citations do not license stills, clips, music, artwork, likenesses, or merchandise.

**Confidence labels are editorial claims, not automatic freshness exemptions.** **Verified** means a dated, official, country-specific check of the claimed episode/player, access tier and subtitle evidence; it expires when that evidence changes or its required recheck is missed. **Listed** means a dated official series or licensed music-storefront listing only, explicitly *not* proof of country-specific playback, tier, episodes or subtitles. **Unknown** means no availability claim; an official link can still help readers check for themselves. The named reviewer (creator or Notion agent for editorial material) records source, check date, territory, claim, next check and expiration/removal action. The current [viewing rules](where-to-watch-operator.md) still require daily checks during an active release and at least every seven days while linked, including qualified listing links; the [soundtrack rules](against-the-current-soundtrack-review.md) require storefront rechecks at least every seven days. A lower-touch cadence for Listed would require an explicit operator-approved maintenance-policy change **before** using it; this brief grants none.

## Review clock and gates

- **Companion windows are separate, not cumulative:** day 30 `[2026-09-29, 2026-10-29)`, day 60 `[2026-10-29, 2026-11-28)`, day 90 `[2026-11-28, 2026-12-28)` UTC. Review a window **after** it closes; wait the full follow-up for 7-/30-day returns. At day 30 choose which existing path to fix and whether the one-pick proposal has evidence; at day 60 reassess source freshness, maintenance hours and repeat use; at day 90 decide continue, narrow or stop rather than auto-expand. The separate Search Console baseline dates from August 30, so its 30/60/90 reviews have a different clock; do not conflate SEO impressions with companion sessions.
- Use the [companion scorecard](c-drama-companion-scorecard.md)'s authorized production aggregates, not local or preview events. `qualifiedSessionVisits` counts session proxies, not unique people. Suppressed `null` is **not zero**. Saves, cohort returns and path-attributed paid purchases remain **not measured** without approved instrumentation and definitions. Verify live Stripe invoice delivery before calling `firstPaidCollectorInvoices` a confirmed outcome; even then it is all Collector acquisition, not this pilot's purchases. If denominators or owner are unavailable, extend or narrow the experiment instead of declaring a winner.
- **For every proposed item before it reaches readers:** record the named reviewer/maintenance owner and check date; official/source-specific URL and exact claimed territory, tier, episode/subtitle window or catalog role; its Verified/Listed/Unknown label and any conflicting facts; planned recheck and expiration/removal action; permissible link/text/media rights; and spoiler boundary across **body and surrounding UI**: title, URL slug, breadcrumbs, continuation links, preview cards, social image and OG title/description/copy. Editorial approval, source verification and publication are distinct. Never infer country playback from a listing, copy fan OST translations as licensed credits, or expose unreleased Archive media as a recommendation. Use the existing [viewing maintenance rules](where-to-watch-operator.md), [soundtrack review](against-the-current-soundtrack-review.md), and [companion approval packet](c-drama-companion-review-packet.md).
