**Purpose:** prepare the account for the first-watch journal without announcing
*The Untamed* before the spoiler firewall and submission path are ready.

**Cadence:** one post per day for five normal posts, one bridge post, then one
announcement/first-entry post when the launch gate is green. These are drafts
for the existing PLAN workflow; nothing here publishes automatically.

- Use the existing *Nian Wushuang* series imagery for the two normal reaction
  posts. Do not add plot claims, cast claims, or copied promotional copy.
- Use the approved Vibe Atlas / LG · 01 artwork for the archetype-grid post.
- Use original Vibe Atlas copy for the “Newcomers see / Veterans know” card.
- Do not mention *The Untamed* in Days 1–6.
- The final announcement remains **HOLD** until the public sealed-submission
  flow is live and the first Episode 1 capture can happen immediately.
- Keep the same post packet available for Rednote and Weibo; platform-specific
  edits can happen in the cross-platform handoff.
