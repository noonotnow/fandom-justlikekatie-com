# The Untamed: three characters, three performers

**Pre-watch / episode 0 · Names only · The 2019 Chinese live-action series**

A name in dialogue belongs to a character. A name in the cast credits belongs to the performer. This small key matches three characters to their performers without opening a recap. It explains the two leads' ordinary personal and courtesy names, then adds one names-only entry for Wen Qing. It includes no plot or relationship explanation.

## First, which Untamed?

Here, **The Untamed (2019)** means the Chinese live-action drama **陈情令 — Chen Qing Ling**. The original series has **50 episodes**. It is not **UNTAMED (2025)**, the English-language Netflix thriller starring Eric Bana, or another work with a similar title.

**Mo Dao Zu Shi (魔道祖师)** is the novel by **Mo Xiang Tong Xiu**, published in English as **Grandmaster of Demonic Cultivation: Mo Dao Zu Shi**. The novel inspired both animation and the live-action drama; they are different versions, not interchangeable episode guides. The performers below are the live-action actors, not a list of animation voice actors. The 20-episode **The Untamed Special Edition** is also a different edit: use the original 50-episode numbering for this companion's Field Journal.

## The two leads: character names and performers

| Character name used in listings | Personal name | Courtesy name | Live-action performer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wei Wuxian (魏无羡)** | **Wei Ying (魏婴)** — Wei is the surname; Ying is the personal name | **Wuxian (无羡)** — with the surname, Wei Wuxian | **Xiao Zhan (肖战)** |
| **Lan Wangji (蓝忘机)** | **Lan Zhan (蓝湛)** — Lan is the surname; Zhan is the personal name | **Wangji (忘机)** — with the surname, Lan Wangji | **Wang Yibo (王一博)** |

For these two entries, the personal-name and courtesy-name forms refer to the same character. **Wei Ying and Wei Wuxian are not two performers. Lan Zhan and Lan Wangji are not two performers.** Xiao Zhan and Wang Yibo are the people playing those characters.

## Why can one character have two names?

The distinction here is between a **personal name** and a **courtesy name (字, zi)**, another name used in address. Keep the surname attached when comparing the forms: **Wei + Ying / Wuxian**, and **Lan + Zhan / Wangji**. This key explains those two pairs only; it is not a rule that every unfamiliar name in the drama is a courtesy name.

English listings may space the same names differently: **Wei Wu Xian** for Wei Wuxian, **Lan Wang Ji** for Lan Wangji, and **Wang Yi Bo** for Wang Yibo. Those spaces do not introduce another character or actor. Who uses a particular form of address can matter in a scene, but this pre-watch key does not interpret it.
