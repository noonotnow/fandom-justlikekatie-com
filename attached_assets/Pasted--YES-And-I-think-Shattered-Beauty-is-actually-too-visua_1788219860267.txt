## My vote: **Hurting for Three Lifetimes**

Chinese label:

> **三生都在心碎**
> 

Supporting line:

> **The costume changes. The suffering does not.**
> 

That is much clearer than Shattered Beauty. It tells the curator this is a **cross-character romantic-devastation board**, not a collection of handsome dark costumes.

Other strong options:

- **Heartbroken Across Three Lifetimes**
- **Love Ruined Him Beautifully**
- **Professionally Devastated**
- **Born to Suffer Beautifully**
- **Grief Has a Face Card**
- **Three Lifetimes of Emotional Damage**

But **Hurting for Three Lifetimes** feels the most Vibe Atlas: thirsty, trope-aware, and specific enough to build around.

## The character clusters become the actual evidence

### Murong Jinghe — 春花焰

Promise:

> Wounded prince, fury curdled into grief, carrying the person whose loss has spiritually ended him.
> 

Visual anchors:

- Blood and visible injury
- Red/black robes
- Disabled or physically vulnerable moments
- Grief turning into rage
- Carrying Mei Lin
- Wedding/funeral imagery
- Kneeling, collapsing, holding, refusing to let go
- The expression of someone whose revenge plan has become a personal curse

That scene where he carries her in the wedding clothes is not merely “a sad picture.” It is an entire **scene-family Event**:

> **Murong Jinghe carrying home the love that destroyed him**
> 

If there are multiple angles, frames, close-ups, and aftermath images, that can absolutely become one Event board.

### Shen Zaiye — 桃花映江山

Promise:

> The immaculate political operator finally made dirty, bloody, desperate, and emotionally compromised.
> 

Visual anchors:

- Bloodied or distressed Shen Zaiye
- Dirty/torn robes
- Hair no longer immaculate
- Crying or restrained devastation
- Carrying or protecting Jiang Taohua
- Political composure visibly cracking
- Injury, sacrifice, exhaustion
- A man discovering that strategy cannot save him from feelings

This cluster is different from Murong Jinghe:

- **Murong Jinghe:** feral grief, revenge, red/black heat
- **Shen Zaiye:** controlled heartbreak, dirty elegance, political restraint breaking

They support the same larger Vibe, but they should not be treated as one visual family.

You could even arrange the 3×3 by “lifetimes,” with each row representing one character cluster.

## Strong internal promise contract

> Liu Xueyi portraying visible romantic devastation across character lifetimes. Cards must show emotional or physical fracture—not merely dark styling, costume drama, or a serious expression. Each image must belong to a named character/scene cluster involving grief, injury, sacrifice, separation, desperate protection, or catastrophic love.
> 

### Hard anti-anchors

- Generic black-robed portraits
- Smiling character stills
- Clean neutral promotional images
- “He looks serious” with no narrative distress
- Modern suits
- Random armor
- Collages
- Red clothing with no heartbreak context
- Another person suffering while Liu Xueyi merely occupies the thumbnail
