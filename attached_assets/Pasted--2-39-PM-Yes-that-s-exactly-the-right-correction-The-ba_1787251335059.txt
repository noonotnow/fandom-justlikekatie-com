sam frodo tired mordor

Those are caption concepts, not retrieval terms.
The rule
Search for the canonical visual evidence. Mutate the caption, not the image query.
So for “Sam and Frodo funny,” the system can think:
supportive contradiction / loyal friend / overprepared Sam
​
But the search should become:
sam frodo tired mordor
sam frodo exhausted still
sam carrying frodo mount doom

Better reactionImageBrief split

visualMeaning: "Sam and Frodo visibly exhausted but still together"

  "sam frodo tired mordor",
  "sam and frodo mordor still",
  "sam frodo exhausted lord of the rings",
  "sam carrying frodo mount doom"

full joke paraphrase;
