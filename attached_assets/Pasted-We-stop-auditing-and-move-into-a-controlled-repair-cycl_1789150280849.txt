We stop auditing and move into a **controlled repair cycle**. Not a grand rewrite; four separable interventions, each tested against the audit baseline.

## 1. Preserve the baseline and create regression fixtures

Before touching behavior, have Replit freeze:

- the 85 audited runs;
- the 20-image blind promise sample and hidden outcome key;
- the Cold Jade counterfactual;
- examples of exact retrieval recurrence;
- representative 4–8-image families;
- examples of generic batches beating expressive ones.

These become permanent regression fixtures. Otherwise the evidence that justified the repair quietly evaporates—the traditional software-development magic trick.

## 2. Make two immediate, low-risk corrections

These are defects rather than unresolved product-policy questions:

### Make refresh genuinely fresh

A repetition-triggered refresh must bypass the one-hour application cache and record:

- whether bypass was requested;
- whether it occurred;
- whether the returned digest set changed.

### Stop calling rearrangement freshness

Reordering the same nine photographs may be a layout recovery, but it cannot count as fresh evidence. Preserve it only as an explicitly identified fallback.

These changes should receive focused tests and can proceed without redesigning promise scoring.

## 3. Improve the candidate supply before tightening repetition rules

Do **not** lower the seven-of-nine threshold yet. A stricter threshold applied to the same depleted pool could simply create more failures and rescue boards.

Instead:

- expand shallow three-query pairings;
- give queries distinct visual jobs;
- measure unique visual yield by query and rung;
- retain a novelty reserve outside the top three batches;
- prevent raw batch size from automatically defeating smaller expressive batches;
- preserve enough candidate receipts to inspect future losses.

A query ladder should deliberately explore mechanisms such as:

- face/expression;
- posture and gesture;
- wardrobe and styling;
- architecture/environment;
- event/editorial coverage;
- scene or character context;
- full-body silhouette;
- motion or interaction.

Not every Vibe Pack needs exactly those jobs, but each rung should explore a different visual route rather than repeatedly asking Google the same question in a slightly different hat.

## 4. Replace the promise cliff with layered evidence

Do not simply remove `coreSatisfied`. Change its authority.

### Proposed behavior

- Metadata-based promise logic remains a retrieval/ranking signal.
- Failure does **not** automatically mean visual failure.
- Metadata-poor candidates receive visual evaluation.
- Candidates are classified as:
    - core;
    - supporting;
    - connective;
    - contradictory;
    - irrelevant;
    - unusable.
- The complete board receives a separate promise assessment.
- Only contradiction, irrelevance, unusability, or failed board-level coherence should exclude an otherwise valid image.

The 20-image sample should be used as the first regression test: the nine known core false negatives must no longer disappear solely because their metadata is sparse.

## 5. Add the missing board composition mode

Keep three explicit strategies:

1. **Compiled**
Independent evidence assembled across contexts.
2. **Event**
A single coherent family can supply the complete board when genuinely rich enough.
3. **Dominant family + support**
A coherent family supplies roughly 4–8 distinct frames, completed by compatible supporting or connective images.

Do not hard-code the final family minimum from intuition alone. Replay the 120 detected families and compare candidate thresholds. The Cold Jade six-plus-three board is a strong fixture, not necessarily the universal formula.

Within coherent-family modes:

- collapse exact photographs and true transformations;
- allow distinct frames from the same shoot;
- judge redundancy by contribution, pose, expression, composition, and narrative beat;
- do not penalize shared wardrobe, lighting, setting, or editorial identity merely for being shared.

## 6. Test each intervention separately

Use shadow generation or offline replay before production.

Compare:

- current behavior;
- cache-bypass only;
- expanded queries;
- expanded candidate reserve/ranking;
- recalibrated promise logic;
- dominant-family mode;
- combined candidate system.

Measure:

- unique image yield;
- historical overlap;
- exact-board recurrence;
- useful candidates surviving each funnel stage;
- promise false negatives on the blind fixture;
- rescue/fallback frequency;
- source and provider concentration;
- human preference between old and counterfactual boards.

This separation tells you which change actually helps.

## 7. Then set freshness policy

After the pool improves, review real counterfactual boards and decide:

- acceptable repeated-card count;
- pairing rotation window;
- when refresh becomes mandatory;
- when a rescue board is acceptable;
- whether same-shoot reuse is valid for the selected board mode;
- what must be disclosed privately in the receipt.

My provisional invariant would be:

> An exact prior board cannot be classified as fresh merely because its cards were rearranged.
> 

Everything more granular should come from the replay results.
