## The important model correction

There are at least two legitimate board modes:

### Evidence-diverse board

The board argues for a Vibe using images from different settings, sources, shoots, and visual mechanisms.

Here, same-shoot concentration may genuinely weaken the board.

### Cohesive event/editorial board

The event, shoot, campaign, or editorial is itself the organizing visual artifact.

Here, multiple images from the same family may be desirable—provided that each image contributes a distinct pose, expression, composition, detail, or narrative beat.

In this mode, forcing source diversity can actively destroy aesthetic coherence.

The system should not decide whether same-shoot repetition is good or bad without knowing **what kind of board it is constructing**.

## Promise fulfillment also needs two levels

An individual image should not necessarily have to express the entire Vibe Pack independently.

- **Core evidence:** images that make the board’s central promise immediately legible.
- **Supporting evidence:** images that reinforce one dimension—setting, wardrobe, gesture, texture, status, atmosphere, expression, or narrative continuity.
- **Connective evidence:** images that may be weaker in isolation but make a sequence or editorial family feel coherent.
- **Contradictory evidence:** images that materially undermine the promised Vibe.

A board can fulfill its promise collectively even when every card is not a tiny self-contained thesis statement wearing excellent trousers.
