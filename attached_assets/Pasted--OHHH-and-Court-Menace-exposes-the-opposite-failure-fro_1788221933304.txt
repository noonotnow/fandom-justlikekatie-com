## The promise needs a stricter contract

**Court Menace** is already a very good public name. Internally, it should mean:

> Liu Xueyi portraying elegant historical characters whose danger comes through political authority, strategic control, social position, or institutional power—not merely weapons, dark clothing, or villainous expressions.
> 

### Required anchors

- Court, official, princely, ministerial, or aristocratic context
- Visible authority or strategic control
- Calculating, assessing, or commanding expression
- Costuming that communicates rank or institutional position
- An implied consequence: this person can order, manipulate, expose, imprison, or politically ruin someone

### Supporting anchors

- Documents, books, seals, desks, fans, thrones
- Palace or official interiors
- Ornate dark green, teal, black, or restrained gold
- Another character visibly under scrutiny
- Controlled physical proximity
- Calmness while everyone else is in danger

### Anti-anchors

- Generic black-costume fantasy
- Battle imagery with no court context
- Soft white immortals
- Modern suits
- Romantic intimacy without visible power or threat
- “He has a sword” as the entire argument
- Collages, subtitles, and platform overlays
- Random villain portraits that lack institutional authority
