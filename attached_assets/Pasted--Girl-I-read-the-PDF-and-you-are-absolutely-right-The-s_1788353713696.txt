# The Star of the Day/release-inventory mess

There were actually **two separate failures** in that Replit exchange.

## 1. Deployment source drift

The work existed on Replit’s `main`, but Netlify was reading GitHub’s `main`, which was behind.

That means “implemented,” “merged,” and “live” were being treated as if they meant the same thing when they did not.

You need three explicit states:

- **Implemented in task workspace**
- **Merged into repository source of truth**
- **Deployed to production**

A task should never be described as available on your site until the deployed Netlify revision is verified.

## 2. The inventory was put in the wrong conceptual location

The inventory existed inside **Actor preflight**, while Admin opened **PLAN schedule**.

Your expectation was reasonable. Release Inventory governs what can ship; it is not merely another actor-calibration diagnostic.

Making Actor Preflight the universal Admin landing screen makes the inventory visible, but it **papers over the information-architecture problem**. Release Inventory should ideally be:

- Its own Operator Console subtab, **or**
- A persistent top-level section available across PLAN schedule and Actor preflight

It should not depend on knowing that “release inventory” secretly lives inside “actor preflight.”

- Whether the curator can regenerate a board
