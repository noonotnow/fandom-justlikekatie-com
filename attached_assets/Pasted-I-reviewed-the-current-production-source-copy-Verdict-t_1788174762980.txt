- **Vibe Atlas 氛围图鉴**

That is the product voice.

## Must-fix: Vibe Atlas introduction

Current copy becomes procedural:

> “set the aesthetic assignment… through that assignment… not choosing an arbitrary actor × vibe pairing”
> 

The final sentence especially sounds like a terms-of-service clarification for a feature nobody accused of misconduct.

Replace the paragraph with:

> **Every day, Vibe Atlas pairs one C-drama star with one Vibe Pack, then searches their iconic characters, looks, and moments for nine cards worth keeping. Browse today’s drop, save your favorites, and build a 3×3 from the evidence.**
> 

It explains the mechanics without exposing the algorithm-training strategy.

## Must-fix: archive copy

Current:

> Revisit a finished daily drop without choosing an arbitrary pairing.
> 

Replace with:

> **Revisit past stars, vibes, and evidence.**
> 

No one needs to be reminded of a feature they cannot use.

## Improve: grid heading

Current:

> Fresh 3×3 evidence grid
Collectible receipts from today’s star × vibe assignment.
> 

Better:

> **Today’s evidence**
Nine cards from today’s star × Vibe Pack.
> 

“Fresh 3×3 evidence grid” contains three nouns competing for custody.

Collection · Grid Builder

Daily discovery stays free. Founding Members can sync collections across devices, build grids from saved cards, and export premium share cards.

**Vibe Atlas page:** 8/10
**Core concept:** clear and distinctive
