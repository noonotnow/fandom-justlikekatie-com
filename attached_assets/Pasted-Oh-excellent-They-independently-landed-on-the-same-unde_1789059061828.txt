## The important findings

The likely failure chain is now concrete:

2. Generic queries return larger, more stable pools.
3. The ranking system rewards result volume and domain count.
4. Smaller, more expressive query batches can be dropped or outranked.
5. Only three top batches survive for later selection.
6. “Refresh” may hit the same one-hour Baidu cache.
7. Repetition is tolerated until seven of nine images overlap.
8. An exact repeated set can be rearranged and treated as recovery.
