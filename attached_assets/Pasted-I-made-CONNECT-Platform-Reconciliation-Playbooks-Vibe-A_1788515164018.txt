I made [CONNECT — Platform Reconciliation Playbooks & Vibe Atlas Metrics Plan](https://app.notion.com/p/CONNECT-Platform-Reconciliation-Playbooks-Vibe-Atlas-Metrics-Plan-b6a26c1fbe0e41228467a67153672ced?pvs=21) under the Social Media Manager Dashboard. It includes the shared reconciliation contract, Rednote-specific rules, Vibe Atlas metadata proposal, rate formulas, weekly manifest procedure, completion report, and open schema decisions.

On Replit’s copy: **the strategy is right, but the rewrite sands off the funniest and most distinctive part.**

### What Replit gets right

- Keep **“One star. One vibe. Nine pieces of evidence.”**
- Use the Pokémon line as the hook, not the entire explanation.
- Follow it with plain-language product copy.
- Remove public references to **Studio Operations**.
- Put Katie/Just Like Katie attribution outside the hero.
- Don’t repeat “Pokémon” throughout the site or treat it as owned SEO language.

### Where I disagree

> You want to collect them all.
> 

That is accurate—but generic and slightly committee-approved. It also echoes **“Gotta catch ’em all”** more closely while somehow being less memorable.

> You wanna catch all these.
> 

That has:

- the thirsty double meaning;
- your actual voice;
- more distance from the official slogan;
- the feeling of someone gesturing toward an alarming display case of attractive men.

I would keep **your line**.

I also don’t love:

> Meet a new C-drama heartthrob archetype every day.
> 

The daily unit is really an **actor × Vibe Pack assignment**, not necessarily a wholly new archetype. And “build your definitive 3×3 with Vibe Atlas Grid Builder” sounds like product documentation wandered into the punchline.

## My preferred hero

> **One star. One vibe. Nine pieces of evidence.**
*Like Pokémon, but thirsty. You wanna catch all these.*
Every day, Vibe Atlas pairs one C-drama star with one very specific kind of heartthrob energy. Browse nine collectible pieces of evidence, save the ones that understand your type, and build your own 3×3.
> 

Then use a clear CTA:

> **Browse today’s drop**
**Open the Grid Builder**
> 

That preserves the elegant thesis, lands the joke, and explains the real behavior without forcing every branded noun into one sentence.

For attribution, I’d use:

> **Fandom Vibes is a creative universe by Katie Hendley, creator of Just Like Katie.**
Vibe Atlas is its daily C-drama collectible.
> 

So my verdict is: **Replit diagnosed the hierarchy correctly but over-domesticated the copy.** Keep the structure; restore the gremlin.
