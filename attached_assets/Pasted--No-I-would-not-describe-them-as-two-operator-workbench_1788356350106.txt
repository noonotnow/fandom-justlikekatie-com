**No—I would not describe them as two operator workbenches.** There should be **one Fandom Vibes Operator Console** with two distinct task environments:

- **Release Desk** — the default operating home
- **Actor Preflight Lab** — the specialized inspection and approval environment

Think **one workplace, two rooms**, not two products.

## I would not put Preflight in a drawer

Replit’s drawer suggestion overcorrects. Preflight contains:

- Dozens of retained images
- Event and Compiled comparisons
- Rescue-board construction
- Query and rejection evidence
- Receipts and human confirmations

That needs a full-width environment. Stuffing it into a drawer would make serious editorial work feel subordinate and cramped.

Instead:

```
Fandom Vibes
└── Operator Console
    ├── Release Desk — default
    ├── Actor Preflight Lab
    ├── Field Journal
    └── Court Rulings
```

Release Desk and Preflight are **not duplicate inventory workspaces**:

### Actor Preflight Lab

> Should this actor × Vibe pairing produce an approved edition?
> 

It retrieves, evaluates, builds, rescues, and freezes the exact nine.

### Release Desk

> What approved edition is ready to ship, and what still blocks it?
> 

It handles enhancement, final assets, copy, repeat warnings, scheduling, publication, holds, and retirement.

The handoff should be direct:

> Preflight approval → automatically appears in Release Desk
> 

And from Release Desk:

> Open preflight receipt → full-page Preflight Lab → return to the same release item
>
