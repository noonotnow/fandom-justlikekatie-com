## What changed strategically

Before this update, there were two possible failure modes:

1. **Generator problem:** CREATE’s caption system is too shallow/templatey.
2. **Context problem:** CREATE’s generator is strong, but Fandom sends it a thin manifest.

Replit confirmed it is mostly #2.

That is excellent, because context/data-contract problems are usually much cheaper to fix than “make the AI magically write better.”

## The new actual product thesis

The thing to build is not “better captions.”

It is:

> **Better creative context at the moment of handoff.**
> 

Fandom knows why the grid exists. CREATE needs to know that too.

Right now CREATE sees roughly:

- packet title
- notes/context
- platform
- format
- tone hint
- per-image role/notes/title/actor/vibe

But it does **not** reliably see:

- search spell
- vibe subtitle
- edition tier
- caption seeds
- CTA seed
- grid/export context
- why these 9 images belong together
- whether this was a daily atlas grid vs saved collection grid
- what aesthetic/family/lens produced the group
- what was manually swapped or deliberately kept
- whether this resembles previously exported grids

So of course the copy can still feel a little samey. The model is being handed ingredients without the little sticky note that says, “This is for emotionally unwell Rednote girlies who love haunted court elegance and one specific man’s microexpression.”

Tragic. Preventable.

## The compounding fix is clear

The next move really should be **Collection Grid Builder**, because it does two jobs at once:

### Job 1: Reduce time-to-post-ready-card

It gives you:

- saved collection as source material
- proposed 3×3
- quick slot swaps
- export
- packet anchor
- CREATE handoff

That is the two-minute studio.

### Job 2: Generate the missing handoff context

The Grid Builder’s `rationale` is not just explanatory UI. It becomes the creative brief for CREATE.

That’s the important part.

A good `rationale` can tell CREATE:

- what visual family unifies the grid
- what emotional stance the grid has
- why these images belong together
- what contrast or progression exists across the 9 slots
- what star/vibe/spell lens shaped the selection
- what caption family would fit
- whether the grid reads as soft, dramatic, archival, funny, romantic, icy, chaotic, etc.

That is exactly the context that makes AI copy stop sounding like a template.

## Tiny caution

I would *not* let this become a huge visual-family taxonomy project yet.

For V1, “visual family” can be inferred crudely from what already exists:

- actor
- vibe
- search spell
- image hash/dedup proximity
- editorialDetection scoring
- saved/exported status
- possibly source batch/date

The danger is spending two weeks making a perfect ontology when the actual win is:

> show Katie a good proposed 3×3 from saved material and give CREATE enough context to write better copy.
> 

No marble cathedral. Raccoon studio first.
