**YES—that wrinkle is actually the breakthrough.** A character cannot belong to only one Vibe Pack.

*Love Never Fails* is **落花时节又逢君**. Liu Xueyi plays **锦绣 / Jinxiu**, a flower deity whose original form is a golden peony; Hongning is the flower spirit, and their story explicitly involves a thousand-year bond and rebirth.[[1]](https://dramatea88.wordpress.com/2025/05/23/love-never-fails-chinese-drama-review/)[[2]](https://baike.baidu.com/en/item/Love%20Never%20Fails/936250)

## Jinxiu belongs to two promises

### Jinxiu’s default character look

- Aloof celestial authority
- Pale robes
- Controlled expression
- Divine distance
- Clean, cool immortal silhouette
- “Feelings are beneath me” posture

That is absolutely **Cold Jade Immortal**.

### Jinxiu’s emotional-breakdown arc

- Following Hongning through reincarnation
- Carrying or protecting her through realms
- Divine composure cracking
- Love becoming punishment, devotion, and redemption
- The aloof god discovering that detachment has consequences

That belongs to the heartbreak pack.

So the hierarchy needs to be:

> **Actor → Character → Look → Emotional/scene state → Vibe compatibility**
> 

Not:

> **Jinxiu = Cold Jade forever**
> 

## Same visual look, different narrative meaning

An image of Jinxiu simply standing there being pale and divine:

> **Cold Jade Immortal — strong match**
Heartbreak — weak/no match
> 

An image of that same pale divine Jinxiu carrying Hongning across whatever infernal customer-service department they were traversing:

> **Heartbreak — strong match**
Cold Jade Immortal — supporting visual match
> 

The costume can remain cold-immortal while the **scene action and emotional state change the primary promise**.

That means an image can have:

- **Visual compatibility**
- **Narrative compatibility**
- **Primary Vibe**
- **Secondary Vibe**

## We should not call the broad pack “Hurting for Three Lifetimes”

You’re right: that phrase points too literally toward Jinxiu’s reincarnation story. It would make the curator over-index on *Love Never Fails* and treat Murong Jinghe and Shen Zaiye as metaphorical substitutes.

Better distinction:

### Broad cross-character Vibe Pack

**Love Ruined Him Beautifully**

Possible Chinese:

> **为爱碎得很漂亮**
> 

This can include:

- Murong Jinghe carrying Mei Lin
- Shen Zaiye dirty, bloody, or emotionally broken
- Jinxiu following Hongning through lifetimes
- Any other Liu Xueyi character visibly destroyed by romantic attachment

The promise is not reincarnation. It is:

> **This beautiful man has been emotionally demolished by love, and we have visual evidence.**
> 

### Jinxiu-specific Event

Within that Vibe Pack:

> **He Followed Her Through Every Lifetime**
> 

or:

> **Carrying Her Through Every Realm**
> 

That is the literal *Love Never Fails* character/scene Event.

## Jinxiu cluster model

**Character:** 锦绣 / Jinxiu
**Drama:** 落花时节又逢君 / *Love Never Fails*

Subclusters:

1. **Aloof Flower Deity**
    - Primary: Cold Jade Immortal
    - Secondary: Courtly divine authority
2. **Divine Composure Cracking**
    - Primary: Love Ruined Him Beautifully
    - Secondary: Cold Jade Immortal
3. **Across-Lifetimes Devotion**
    - Primary: Love Ruined Him Beautifully
    - Event subtype: character/scene arc
4. **Carrying Hongning Through the Realms**
    - Primary: Love Ruined Him Beautifully
    - Event subtype: iconic scene family
    - Strong narrative anchor even if the palette remains pale and celestial

## This is why placement matters too

A Cold Jade board could include **one** image of devastated Jinxiu as an emotional crack in the ice. That strengthens the board.

But if six cards show him carrying, crying, kneeling, and pursuing Hongning, the board is no longer primarily about aloof immortality. It has become:

> **The flower god has discovered consequences.**
> 

So yes: this is not a taxonomy problem to eliminate. It is exactly the nuance the character-cluster system must preserve.

**Characters have baseline aesthetics. Scenes have emotional states. Vibe Packs interpret the combination.** Jinxiu can be Cold Jade while standing alone and Love Ruined Him Beautifully the moment he picks her up and starts hauling his catastrophic feelings across metaphysical jurisdictions.
