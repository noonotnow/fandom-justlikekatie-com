## What this teaches Cold Jade Immortal

### Required visual anchors

- White, silver, gray, or ice-blue robes
- Restrained or distant expression
- Celestial/historical styling
- Clean, elegant silhouette
- Cool or muted environment
- Stillness, authority, or emotional reserve

### Supporting anchors

- Pale outdoor light
- Mist, moonlight, water, stone, or greenery
- Long sleeves and robe movement
- Hair ornaments or divine regalia
- Controlled close-ups
- Wider costume-study frames

### Anti-anchors

- Saturated red as the dominant color
- Warm gold courtliness
- Smiling or overtly playful energy
- Generic dark-costume intensity
- Modern portraiture
- Collages, posters, or text-heavy edits
- Emotional collapse that belongs in **Professionally Devastated**
