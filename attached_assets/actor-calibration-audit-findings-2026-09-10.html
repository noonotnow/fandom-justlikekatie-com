Finding 1 — promise miscalibration is substantial

Of 1,367 candidate placements carrying a promise decision, 934 (68.3%) failed coreSatisfied and 433 passed.

A disjoint, stratified blind sample was classified visually before its hidden outcome key was opened. Among 20 promise failures:

  • 9 core images directly fulfilled the Vibe.
  • 5 supporting images strengthened a board without carrying its thesis.
  • 2 connective images could bridge the dominant family to supporting material.
  • 3 irrelevant and 1 unusable were appropriate failures.

Result: 16 of 20 rejected images were visually useful, including 9 direct false negatives. This is a stratified proof sample, not a population-weighted error estimate.

Finding 2 — retrieval repetition is also real

  • There were 61 consecutive same-pair run comparisons. Ten returned the exact same digest set; thirteen overlapped by at least 70%.
  • All ten exact repeats used the same query set. Eight occurred within roughly 45 seconds or concurrently.
  • The export has no cache-hit flag, so this proves repeated retrieval output but cannot distinguish provider stability from an in-process cache.
  • Google Images supplied 314 of 321 query batches. The top five result domains accounted for 44.2% of retained candidate placements.
  • Several heavily repeated pairings used only three to six unique queries across many runs. Liu Xueyi / Polished Danger produced 791 placements across 22 runs but only 37 unique image digests.

Finding 4 — the strict nine-image Event requirement excludes a real board class

The export contains 120 detected visual families of size 4–8 across 65 runs. Every one occurred in a run with at least nine non-dropped raw candidates. This does not prove every family can anchor a publishable board, but it establishes a large counterfactual opportunity set.

The retained Cold Jade run below contains a seven-image coherent pale-robed family. Six distinct family frames plus three compatible pale/cool supports form a complete visual argument without pretending all nine are one detected family.

Conclusion

Retrieval scarcity/repetition: confirmed. Repeated queries often return identical sets, and substantial clean result volume sits behind the accepted-batch boundary.

Misapplied promise logic: confirmed. Blind review found direct and supporting false negatives across many actor/Vibe pairs.

Dominant-family plus support: supported. The data contains many 4–8-image families, and the Cold Jade counterfactual demonstrates a coherent nine-card composition.

Do not collapse these into one fix. Query/cache diagnostics, promise calibration, and Event composition should remain separate interventions with separate tests.

What remains unobservable

  • Never-retrieved images cannot appear in this export.
  • Cache hits were not historically retained.
  • Rank 4–10 result links and images were generally not retained, preventing visual review of the top-three boundary.
  • Explicit pre/post-deduplication ledgers, ranking receipts, edition links, and publication links are absent from all 85 historical runs.
  • The export is an eventually consistent snapshot, although it reported no listing or output truncation.

Safe next diagnostic step

Before changing production behavior, retain future cache-hit markers and candidate links for rejected ranking batches, then repeat the same blind protocol. Separately evaluate dominant-family boards offline against the current Compiled board. No search, ranking, promise, Event, or publication rule should change solely from this historical export.
