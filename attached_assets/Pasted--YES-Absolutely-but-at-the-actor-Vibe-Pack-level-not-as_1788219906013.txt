**YES. Absolutely—but at the `actor × Vibe Pack` level, not as one permanent archetype assigned to the actor.**

That gives us three clean layers:

> **Vibe Pack = the visual promise**
**Character/look cluster = the evidence**
**Archetype = the narrative interpretation**
> 

## Example

### Liu Xueyi × Cold Jade Immortal

- Character evidence: aloof Jinxiu, pale ceremonial Yuan Zhong
- Visual promise: cool, divine, restrained, untouchable
- Game archetype: **The Aloof Immortal** or whichever existing archetype corresponds

### Liu Xueyi × Professionally Devastated

- Character evidence: Murong Jinghe, distressed Shen Zaiye, late-arc Jinxiu
- Visual promise: romantic and physical ruin
- Game archetype: **The Beautifully Doomed One**

### Liu Xueyi × Court Menace

- Character evidence: calculating court roles and dangerous political looks
- Visual promise: elegant institutional threat
- Game archetype: **The Smiling Strategist**, **Court Fox**, or its existing equivalent

Same actor, different cards in the archetype deck.

## Why this is useful

It connects several product systems without conflating them:

- Vibe Atlas grids become evidence for archetype assignments.
- The archetype game becomes a discovery route into actor/Vibe cards.
- Character clusters explain *why* the assignment makes sense.
- One actor can demonstrate several archetypes across roles.
- Users can compare:
    
    > “Liu Xueyi is an Aloof Immortal here, but Professionally Devastated over there.”
    > 
- Exported grids can optionally carry an archetype label.
- Membership profiles can learn which archetypes someone repeatedly saves or builds.

It also gives us extremely good editorial content:

> **One actor. Three characters. Which xianxia archetype owns the face card?**
> 

## Data model

Each approved actor/Vibe pairing could have:

```
Primary archetype
Secondary archetype (optional)
Supporting character clusters
Confidence/status
Editorial rationale
Evidence board
Archetype taxonomy version
```

Possible statuses:

- Suggested by system
- Confirmed by editor
- Confirmed by exported-grid behavior
- Disputed
- Needs more evidence

## Important restraint

The archetype should **not become another curation gate**.

It can help generate queries and interpret the finished board, but the system should not reject an excellent Cold Jade grid because the archetype classifier thinks one card looks 14% too emotionally available.

And the mapping cannot be:

> Liu Xueyi = Aloof Immortal
> 

It must be:

> Liu Xueyi × Cold Jade Immortal = Aloof Immortal
Liu Xueyi × Professionally Devastated = Beautifully Doomed
Liu Xueyi × Court Menace = Elegant Schemer
> 

That is basically **Pokémon typing for C-drama men**. The actor is the card universe, the Vibe Pack is the build, the character clusters are the moveset, and the archetype is the type assignment.

So yes—we want this. It makes the game and Vibe Atlas feel like two views of the same underlying fandom intelligence rather than two cute features living in adjacent apartments.
