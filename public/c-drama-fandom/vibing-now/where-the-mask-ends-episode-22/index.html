One Victory Does Not Make the Palace Safe

A reckoning at court gives way to a search that exposes the limits of power.

Spoilers through Episode 22

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 22. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

The Han family’s position is collapsing. Wei Rao helps Lady Li turn an attempted secret message into a way of tracking where the Empress’s allies go. At court, Prince Rong’s appeal only adds to the disorder. The Emperor strips the Empress and Prince Jing of their ranks, while the Empress Dowager is urged to sacrifice one member of the family to protect the rest.

Wei Rao confronts the deposed Empress about Bi Tao. The woman who dismissed a servant’s life is no longer speaking from the same secure position. It is a moment of reckoning, but it cannot return the person Wei Rao lost.

The court rearranges itself

The Han family is punished, though the final handling of its weapons charge is less severe than the earlier threat of treason suggested. Prince Duan becomes crown prince. Wei Rao has achieved a measure of justice for Bi Tao, yet the title changing hands does not mean the power attached to it has become harmless.

At the estate, Lu Zhuo keeps trying to see Wei Rao and is turned away. Qi Zhongkai manages to enter. After all the court maneuvering, access to Wei Rao’s front door remains its own competitive event.

Hongxian disappears

News arrives that the mother who raised Wei Rao has died, and Hongxian and their brother have not returned to the estate. Hongxian is abducted while selling food. She escapes briefly with another captive and seeks help from Wei Shu, not knowing that the crown princess is involved in the danger. The other girl is killed, and Hongxian is made unable to speak.

The shift is deeply unsettling. Hongxian reaches for a connection to Wei Rao as protection, but the person she trusts is part of the machinery threatening her. Rank and family names can open doors; they can also conceal who controls the room.

A banquet and a search

On the way to the crown prince’s banquet, Lu Zhuo tells Wei Rao he will not force her to honor their earlier understanding and offers protection. Inside, Wei Shu keeps Wei Rao occupied while Hongxian tries to reach her without being able to call out.

Wei Rao and her allies search for Hongxian. Their inquiries lead to a broker who reveals a pattern of missing girls protected by powerful people, and points toward the new crown prince. The episode closes with the danger identified, not resolved.

Episode 22 gives Wei Rao a public victory and immediately exposes its limits. Removing one powerful enemy has not dismantled the system that makes vulnerable women available to the next.
