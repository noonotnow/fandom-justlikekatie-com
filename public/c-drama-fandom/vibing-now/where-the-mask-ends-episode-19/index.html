A Tournament, a Brother and an Extremely Bad Proposal

A contest, a new family connection and a palace full of plans for Wei Rao.

Spoilers through Episode 19

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 19. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

Wei Rao has escaped one marriage, and the palace is already holding a meeting about the next one. The Empress Dowager proposes Han Liao, whose enthusiasm for the arrangement comes with gifts for the Empress. Lady Li counters by making the selection part of a military contest. Apparently a woman saying she does not want to remarry is merely the beginning of the group project.

A new brother arrives

Liu Shuo, Lady Li’s son and Wei Rao’s half-brother, arrives to escort her to the contest. He is eager to establish a connection; she is wary and formally polite. It’s an interesting mismatch in temperature: he approaches her as family, while she has reasons to treat a newly discovered family bond as something to assess, not instantly trust.

Bi Tao has also returned to Wei Rao’s household. Their reunion offers a quiet reminder that the relationships around Wei Rao do not reset neatly just because her position has changed.

Marriage as the prize nobody asked for

At the contest, Han Liao’s advantages include influence, dirty tricks and a worrying enthusiasm for crossbows. Qi Zhongkai is sidelined after drinking drugged wine, and Lu Zhuo’s younger brother is badly beaten. Lu Zhuo, still recovering himself, enters the fight and wins despite taking an arrow.

While the arena is busy trying to decide what Wei Rao’s future should be, she makes an argument of her own: ordinary soldiers should be allowed to compete too. She is not just standing there as a prize while everyone else debates qualifications.

Han Liao miscalculates. Again.

Concerned about Lu Zhuo, Wei Rao follows a message into a room, where Han Liao has arranged a trap meant to compromise her reputation and corner her into marriage. His plan assumes she will stay frightened and helpless. It does not go well for him.

Lu Zhuo finds her after she has fought back, and the two improvise a cover story when the Empress’s party arrives. Their old ability to turn a dangerous situation into a performance is still very much operational, even if their official marriage is not.

The journey home

The palace intrigue turns deadly on the way back. Attackers strike, and Bi Tao is hit by an arrow as Wei Rao returns. The episode ends on that devastating image rather than giving the audience an answer about what happens next.

The contrast is brutal: an episode that starts with other people treating Wei Rao’s marriage like a competition ends by showing the cost of making her life a courtly game.
