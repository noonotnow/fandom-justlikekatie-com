Meet the new focus · Opening premise only

The mask may get her through the door.

Where the Mask Ends begins with a bride swap, a nearly dead husband, and the sort of family arrangement that should probably have required independent legal counsel.

Wei Rao needs someone else to take her place at the wedding. A-Chou has the right face, a family to protect, and very little reason to trust the people offering her a way out. So she steps into the marriage—and into a household full of people who think they already know exactly who she is.

What makes this fun isn’t just the mistaken identity. It’s watching A-Chou learn how to use the role she’s been given.
