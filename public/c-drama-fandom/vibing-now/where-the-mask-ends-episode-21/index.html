The Estate Has a Basement Problem

An estate secret and a family performance bring the struggle to court.

Spoilers through Episode 21

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 21. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

Chi Lan’s death leads Wei Rao, Lu Zhuo and Qi Zhongkai toward a secret beneath the estate. The property once belonged to the Han family, and a servant has remained behind to guard wealth left in a hidden cellar. The discovery connects an apparently isolated death to the unfinished business of the estate’s former owners.

Qi’s first instinct is to use the money to help people. Wei Rao and Lu Zhuo recognize that simply possessing it could make her vulnerable. They move carefully—and the fact that an estate servant is reporting to Han Liao makes careful an excellent idea.

A family quarrel with an audience

At the imperial park, an apparent attack exposes the competing instincts and ambitions of the princes and their mothers. Wei Rao rebukes Liu Shuo, and her clash with Lady Li is conspicuous enough to attract Consort Zheng’s attention. Wei Shu offers sympathy and a charm; Consort Zheng offers to host a reconciliation.

The banquet reveals that the quarrel was a performance. Wei Rao and Lady Li expose a maid planted in Lady Li’s household and a plot involving curse charms. The supposed peace offering becomes an opportunity to force Consort Zheng into a political arrangement.

That is a different kind of family closeness from the easy affection Liu Shuo seemed to want when he first appeared. Here mother, son and daughter coordinate under pressure—but a shared plan is not the same thing as every hurt between them being healed.

Han Liao brings the accusation to court

A servant tells Han Liao about boxes moved at the estate, and he uses her accusation to claim Wei Rao has stolen relief supplies. The search does not deliver the incriminating treasure he expects. Instead, the investigation uncovers hidden armor bearing the Han family’s mark.

It is a spectacular reversal: the man hoping to make the estate’s secrets incriminate Wei Rao has helped bring attention to secrets dangerous to his own family. The amount of confidence he carries into these plans deserves study, if not imitation.

An extremely badly timed omen

As the court deals with the discovery, Prince Jing arrives with news of a supposedly auspicious sight. The Emperor is in no mood to celebrate. By the end of the episode, Prince Jing is stripped of his princely robes, and the Empress is confined.

Wei Rao and Lu Zhuo’s collaboration has produced a real result. But the satisfaction is not just watching a rival’s scheme unravel: it is seeing Wei Rao use information, family alliances and an opponent’s confidence to change who is exposed when the doors are opened.
