The Spring Banquet Was Never About Spring

A face reading, a palace brawl and a trap that turns in Wei Rao’s hands.

Spoilers through Episode 26

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 26. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

Yi Jiang warns that a deadly ordeal lies ahead of Wei Rao, and that unless she survives it she will not live past twenty. Lady Li is furious. Wei Rao laughs it off and says she has eighty years left.

The banquet’s real purpose arrives quickly. Consort Zheng wants Hongxian as the crown prince’s concubine. Wei Rao refuses on the grounds that Hongxian cannot speak, and Wei Shu tries to force Hongxian’s head down so she will bow in thanks.

Two consorts, one floor

Wei Rao asks to speak with Consort Zheng up close and whispers that she is not empress yet and should stop acting like one. Consort Zheng leaps up to slap her, and Wei Rao falls to the floor looking thoroughly wronged. Lady Li slaps Consort Zheng twice, and the two women end up fighting. When the Emperor and Liu Shuo arrive, Lady Li speaks first, and Consort Wang and Xie Hualou both take Wei Rao’s side. Liu Shuo then announces that he wants Hongxian himself, which takes her out of the crown prince’s reach. Consort Wang concludes that Wei Rao has a ruler’s talent and a short life.

Wei Rao does not win the fight with Consort Zheng. She loses it on purpose, in front of the right audience.

Warnings travel fast

In the carriage afterward, Liu Shuo tells Xie Hualou he asked for Hongxian only to protect her, and that if Xie Hualou marries him she will receive every honor due a prince’s wife. Moved, she asks him to warn Wei Rao that Wei Shu has been in contact with refugees from Ancheng. Liu Shuo passes the warning to Lu Zhuo, who takes Yi Jiang’s reading seriously enough to assign Zhao Song and Zhao Bai to follow Wei Rao everywhere. Wei Rao tells A Man to pack money so Hongxian can slip away, but Hongxian refuses to leave her in danger. Wei Rao promises to make the crown prince answer for what he did.

The plague trap

When trouble breaks out at the Zhuyu estate, Lu Zhuo and Qi Zhongkai ride there together. The Ancheng refugees at the estate have the spring plague. Lu Zhuo tries to keep Wei Rao away from a sick little girl, but she touches the child anyway and promises the refugees a sick ward on the back hill, with food, water, herbs and doctors. The refugees pray for her long life. A plot meant to ruin her has made her their protector. The steward who let the refugees in admits a stranger paid him two gold beads, and Lu Zhuo and Qi Zhongkai conclude the Eastern Palace was behind it.

On the wedding day, Liu Shuo comes for Hongxian and promises to treat her well. Wei Rao knows Hongxian and Xiao had feelings for each other, so she lets Xiao help Hongxian into the bridal carriage, a quiet goodbye to something that never had a chance. Then Wei Rao moves to the estate, and Lu Zhuo moves with her. She plans to strike back at everyone pressing in on her. He does not want to hear the plan, but he gives her the use of his people.

Wei Rao laughed off the reading at the start of the banquet. By the end of the episode, everyone around her is acting as if it might be true.
