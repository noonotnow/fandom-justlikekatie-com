Married Again, With Better Paperwork

Same groom, same household, entirely different terms.

Spoilers through Episode 25

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 25. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

In the dark of the eclipse, Wei Rao and Lu Zhuo run toward each other through the crowd. She tells him she does not want to die alone if the sun never comes back, and he promises she will not be alone. They kiss, and the light returns.

Soon after, on an auspicious day, Lu Zhuo marries her again. This time he collects the bride himself, and she enters the Lu household as the Wengzhu of Wu’an in her own right, not as a substitute sent to bring luck to a dying man.

That is the joke in the title and also the point. The first wedding happened to her. This one she chose.

Alfalfa is a love language

On the wedding night, Wei Rao pretends not to care and reads a book about farming. Lu Zhuo is also reading, though his book is considerably more illustrated. She sleeps until noon the next day, and he has already sent someone to apologize to the elders for the missed morning greeting.

Hongxian moves into the Duke’s residence as Wei Rao’s sworn sister, and Wei Rao deliberately frightens Lu Changning out of bullying her. Lu Zhuo has the garden’s flowers dug up and alfalfa planted, a hardy crop that survives cold and drought and fattens horses, after going to some trouble to find the seed for her. In return, she gives him a very sharp sword from her storehouse. He sulks when he learns it was a gift from Qi Zhongkai, and tells her she does not understand love yet; if she ever does, it can only be him.

A body in the well

Servants clearing a dry well behind Songyue Hall find Nanny Wu’s body. Two of the family’s daughters-in-law produce a silver needle as proof of poisoning and all but accuse Wei Rao. Before she can answer, Lu Zhuo takes the blame. He says Nanny Wu tried to steal military reports from his study, was caught, and poisoned herself out of fear. The matter is dropped, and he sends away his cousin He Weiyu for siding with the accusers.

Wei Rao is not relieved. He lied for her and set his principles aside to do it. She sends him to sleep in his study and allows him in her bed only twice a month. He comes back that same night and refuses to leave, and she quietly decides she will never let him go.

Liu Shuo brings word of a spring banquet: Consort Zheng wants to make peace between Wei Rao and the crown prince and crown princess, and Hongxian must attend too. At the banquet, Lady Li introduces Wei Rao to Yi Jiang, Prince Fu’s consort, who reads faces. Reading Wei Rao’s, Yi Jiang sees disaster ahead.

Episode 25 gives the couple a real wedding and then tests it immediately. The first question the marriage faces is whether Lu Zhuo will lie for her. He does, without hesitation, and that is exactly what frightens her.
