Grief Does Not Make Her Obedient

Loss, palace pressure and the question of who gets to decide Wei Rao’s future.

Spoilers through Episode 20

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 20. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

The ambush that ended Episode 19 continues, with Liu Shuo and A Man fighting to protect Wei Rao and Bi Tao. Bi Tao dies in Wei Rao’s arms. Their reunion had barely had time to settle before it was taken away.

The attackers claim to be bandits seeking revenge, but Wei Rao suspects the Empress. For a while she withdraws into grief, and the people around her search for a way to reach her.

Grandmother’s field trip

Lu Zhuo brings Lady Shou’an to the estate, where she gets Wei Rao out into the fields. Lu Zhuo joins in the work. It is an oddly practical intervention: if everyone insists on hovering, they might as well pick up a tool.

Lady Shou’an warns Wei Rao that moving against the Empress and the Han family is dangerous. Wei Rao listens, but the warning does not settle the question of what she wants to do. Concern for her safety and respect for her decision are not always the same thing.

The palace makes its point

When Wei Rao goes to the palace, the Empress humiliates her and strikes her maid. Wei Rao endures the indignity, knowing what open defiance could cost. Lady Li offers a route away from danger, but Wei Rao is not interested in leaving the capital. Her grief for Bi Tao sharpens the distance between her and her birth mother: the person Lady Li sees as a maid was someone Wei Rao held close.

Wei Rao’s apparently wounded exchange with Lady Li and Liu Shuo also attracts the attention of Consort Zheng and Wei Shu. In this palace, even a family argument can acquire an audience with political interests.

A deal in the middle of a very complicated reunion

Qi Zhongkai makes his interest in marrying Wei Rao clear. Lu Zhuo, meanwhile, finds his own way into her room. He kisses her; she slaps him. Their renewed closeness is not a substitute for a conversation about what she wants.

He offers to help her take on the Han family and the Empress, with remarriage part of the bargain. The proposed alliance has terms worth noticing: after an episode of people trying to decide where she should go and whom she should marry, her choice deserves more attention than the men’s competition for it.

Another death at the estate

The household’s brief respite ends with another killing. A maid, Chi Lan, is found dead, and a suspect is caught. Whatever is hidden on this property has become a danger to the people trying to make a life there.

Episode 20 moves from public humiliation to private grief and back again. Wei Rao’s refusal to make Bi Tao’s life small is also a refusal to accept the palace’s idea of whose loss should count.
