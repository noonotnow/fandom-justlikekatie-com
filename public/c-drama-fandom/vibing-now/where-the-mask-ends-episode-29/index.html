Fog Over the Front

A poisoned soup, a war in the north and a household dressed in white.

Spoilers through Episode 29

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 29. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

Han Liao works on Qi Zhongkai’s feelings for Wei Rao and invites him to join forces. Qi Zhongkai turns him down at first, but when Han Liao offers Wei Rao herself as the reward, he drinks the wine Han Liao has poured, and seems to accept.

Meanwhile, Prince Fu learns that Consort Zheng has been putting poisonous gelsemium in the Emperor’s ginseng soup in place of Solomon’s seal, a mix that is especially dangerous alongside the cinnabar in his elixirs. Over Liu Shuo’s objections, Prince Fu takes it to the Emperor, who throws down the pills he has taken for years.

A mother’s last move

The Emperor summons Consort Zheng and the crown prince to Xuanshi Hall. She plays innocent until, without a word, the Emperor has the soup and the pills handed to her son. Rather than let him take them, she swallows everything herself and admits why she did it. She soon coughs up blood. The crown prince cannot look at her and does not plead for her. Moved by how far she went to protect him, the Emperor spares him and keeps him as heir.

The crown prince feels no gratitude, only fear of losing his title. With Wei Shu’s encouragement, he starts to think about taking the throne. Wei Shu goes along with him while privately mocking the idea that he can raise a rebel army and still expect the realm’s loyalty.

The war

Han Liao has been secretly dealing with Di Man, king of the Hutuo. When the Hutuo attack, Lu Zhuo is sent to the front at once, with no time to say goodbye. Wei Rao rides after the army but arrives too late; hills stand between them, and neither can see the other. In camp, Lu Zhuo shuts Han Liao out of the planning, though Han Liao has his informants. Remembering Qi Zhongkai’s advice, Lu Zhuo decides to use the campaign to test Wei Rao’s feelings and sends Zhao Song back to her with a message. Qi Zhongkai ties Zhao Song up before he can deliver it.

Under heavy fog, Lu Zhuo launches a surprise attack, catches Han Liao without his armor and kills Di Man. Then he learns that his cousin Lu Ya has pushed too far and is trapped at Iron Scorpion Ridge. Turning back to rescue him, Lu Zhuo goes over into a gorge.

No tears

The army comes home victorious, and Wei Rao is met with Lu Zhuo’s coffin. The Lu women weep. Wei Rao does not. She goes straight to the Zhuyu estate and tells Xiao to gather everyone. Refugees cannot beat cavalry, so she orders two hundred carrier pigeons instead. The Duke’s house is hung with white, but she wears her own clothes and the ring Lu Zhuo gave her.

The Emperor coughs up blood and collapses. The crown prince, delighted, rides to the Zhuyu estate with his soldiers, finds it empty, senses something is wrong and heads for the palace. Li Wei of the Northern Army is ordered to take Liu Shuo and the women to safety. Yi Jiang reads the signs and tells Prince Fu to wait, so he gathers his private troops and prepares to back whoever wins. At the palace, the crown prince still hopes to get the imperial seal from his father, and Wei Shu finds him laughably foolish.

Episode 29 strips Wei Rao of almost everything that has protected her: the Emperor, the army and Lu Zhuo. What she has left is a ring, a courtyard full of refugees and two hundred birds.
