The Mask Goes Back On

A garden visit, a decoy and a rescue that costs more than anyone planned.

Spoilers through Episode 23

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 23. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

Content note: This recap describes sexual violence. Please read with care.

Wei Rao finds a reason to tour the crown prince’s house, which gives A Man the chance to slip away toward a heavily guarded courtyard. When Wei Rao tries to look inside, Wei Shu stops her and produces Jin Chang, the Wei family steward who knows Wei Rao once sat in a condemned cell. The message is simple: look away, or your past comes out.

On the other side of the door, Hongxian hears her sister. Unable to call out, she strikes her head against the door. With everyone watching, Wei Rao has to leave without her.

It is one of the cruelest scenes the drama has staged. The rescue does not fail for lack of nerve. It fails because Wei Rao’s own history has become the lock on the door.

She sends Lu Zhuo to the wrong house

Afterward, Wei Rao sits for hours in the small courtyard where she grew up, and Lu Zhuo finds her there. She asks whether he or Qi Zhongkai would have looked at her twice if she still hauled night soil. Then she plays helpless about Jin Chang, and Lu Zhuo offers to deal with him, fully aware that he is being worked.

That night she gathers her own men from the Zhuyu estate and points Lu Zhuo and Qi Zhongkai toward the crown prince’s villa in the east of the city. By the time a lookout tells him she has left with her own people, Lu Zhuo understands the assignment was a decoy. He raids the villa anyway. Masked, he and Qi Zhongkai free the people held there. Jin Chang is not among them.

The raid

Wei Rao’s group strikes the crown prince’s house itself. Jin Chang cuts her near the collarbone, and Wei Shu watches the fight from behind a rockery. Wei Rao reaches Hongxian while the crown prince is sexually assaulting her. She takes off her mask so her sister knows her, wraps Hongxian in her fox-fur cloak, and her men free the other kidnapped girls. Before she leaves, Wei Rao maims the crown prince so that he can never father a child.

The mask comes off for one reason: so the person being rescued is not afraid of the person rescuing her. Everything else in the scene is about the danger of being seen.

A bed, a search and an argument

Xiao takes Hongxian to safety while the Northern Army searches the city. Lu Zhuo finds the wounded Wei Rao and brings her back to the Duke’s house. Tipped off by Wei Shu that the attacker is injured, the officer Li Wei forces his way into Lu Zhuo’s room and finds Wei Rao in his bed. The two of them put on a convincing enough scene that Li Wei is reprimanded and leaves without checking her for wounds.

Then the argument they have been avoiding arrives. Wei Rao apologizes, and Lu Zhuo begins to soften, until he realizes she went in expecting not to come back. He tells her he can no longer trust her and walks out.

Episode 23 is about the cost of acting alone. Wei Rao’s plan works, and it nearly takes her with it. Lu Zhuo’s anger is about the deception, and about what the deception revealed: she had not planned to return.
