Seven Days Is a Long Time to Decide

The raid’s consequences reach the palace gate while Wei Rao runs out of time.

Spoilers through Episode 24

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 24. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

Qi Zhongkai reports what the eastern villa held: relatives of the kidnapped girls, including a minor clerk mutilated so that he cannot testify. There is no sign of Jin Chang. Li Wei’s overnight search turns up nothing, and the checkpoints have to come down at dawn.

The scandal goes public

Women gather outside the Duan Gate, crying that powerful men took their daughters. The Emperor presses the chief of justice, who names the crown prince, and the heir is made to kneel outside Xuanshi Hall until he faints. With the scandal hanging over him, he cannot hunt the masked attacker without drawing attention to what was done to him. Liu Shuo hurries to help his brother and offers to plead for him.

The episode lets Wei Rao’s plan do its work in public. The protesters are not a plot device; they are the families the crown prince assumed would never be heard.

Wei Shu suspects who wore the mask, but the crown prince refuses to believe a woman could have done this. Unsatisfied, Wei Shu visits the Zhuyu estate. Wei Rao arranges for Hongxian to walk past, and Wei Shu knocks the teacup out of Hongxian’s hand. Her suspicion is confirmed, but Wei Rao knows she has no proof.

Lady Shou’an sets a deadline

Word spreads through the Duke’s household that Wei Rao spent the night in Lu Zhuo’s Songyue Hall. Lu Zhuo silences the gossip and tells his family to stop raising the subject of marriage. At the estate, Cheng Yu is showing off his improved bookkeeping for Wei Rao when Lady Shou’an arrives unannounced. She sends him off with Huo Jue’s trading caravan and gives Wei Rao seven days to settle things with Lu Zhuo before the Lu family’s name suffers. Wei Rao answers that her first marriage was forced on her and asks to choose for herself this time.

Xie Hualou begs Wei Rao to turn Lu Zhuo down so that she can marry him instead of Prince Fu. Wei Rao refuses. Wei Shu has already approached Xie Hualou, who will not stoop to murder or framing anyone; Wei Shu asks only that she speak well of the crown prince to her grandfather, the Grand Tutor.

The seventh day

On the last day, a cold snap sends Wei Rao to check on her grandmother. Lu Zhuo rides past her carriage on his way home. She does not call out to him, not wanting to drag him down with her.

Then an eclipse darkens the sky. The crowd panics, Wei Rao jumps down to look for him, and he hears her.

Seven days were supposed to force a decision. In the end it takes the sun going out.
