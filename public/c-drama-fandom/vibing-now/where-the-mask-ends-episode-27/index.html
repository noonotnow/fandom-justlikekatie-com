The Estate Closes Ranks

The court leaves the capital, and the people Wei Rao fed decide who stays.

Spoilers through Episode 27

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 27. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

The Emperor visits Consort Zheng, who complains about her humiliation at the banquet. Instead of sympathy, she gets a rebuke: the crown prince’s abduction of women has already outraged the public, and she should stop making trouble on his behalf. She apologizes, and the Emperor promises not to blame her for her son.

At the palace, Xie Hualou now greets Wei Rao as an elder sister, and so does Liu Shuo. He was raised to think of her as his own blood, yet he can never quite get close to her, and he envies how easily Lu Zhuo does. When Wei Rao learns where the Emperor is dining that night, she senses danger. Xie Hualou promises to get word to Prince Fu.

The ward on the back hill

Wei Rao nurses the plague patients herself. Lu Zhuo’s mother brings the women of the Duke’s household to help, and even Lu Changning, who has never liked Wei Rao, is impressed. When the plague reaches the army, Lu Zhuo isolates the sick in time to stop it spreading.

The Emperor leaves

Prince Fu and the crown prince argue over whether the Emperor should escape the plague at the hot-spring palace. Then the Emperor falls ill, takes one of his elixir pills and coughs up blood. He leaves with his consorts, and Lu Zhuo and Qi Zhongkai ride with him as escorts.

After saying goodbye to Lu Zhuo, Wei Rao sends A Man to check on Nanny Liu. As more patients arrive, she sends the Duke’s women home and works at the Zhuyu estate day and night, until she catches the spring plague herself. Before she collapses, she puts Liu Ziyang and Xiao in charge, though only her own people will take orders from them.

The Empress Dowager’s decree

The crown prince seizes Nanny Liu, and under pressure from Wei Shu she agrees to testify against Wei Rao. Han Liao and the crown prince take this to the Empress Dowager and persuade her to move against both Wei Rao and Lady Shou’an at once, before the Emperor returns. Wei Rao calls Xiao in and gives him a task.

The next day, Han Liao storms the estate with the Empress Dowager’s decree and drags Wei Rao out, sick and barefoot, in front of everyone. The Dowager’s procession arrives with Wei Shu and the crown prince and rolls straight over the grain the villagers have spread out to dry. Lu Zhuo hears that Wei Rao is ill and rides back from the hot-spring palace.

Lady Shou’an and Huo Jue stand in front of Wei Rao. Lady Shou’an accuses Wei Shu of slandering her own uncle’s only child, and then kneels to plead for her. The Dowager enjoys the sight and has her detained too. Wei Shu suggests putting Wei Rao in a cangue and parading her through the streets. The crowd weeps and protests: the rich who trample the poor live in comfort, while the people who work the land go hungry. To them Wei Rao is almost a goddess, and they will not let Han Liao take her.

Everyone with power in Episode 27 either leaves or abuses it. The people who stand their ground are the ones Wei Rao kept alive.
