Nobody Takes the Wengzhu

A crowd with farm tools, a carriage with a surprise inside and a wager with a life at stake.

Spoilers through Episode 28

Spoiler boundary: This installment stops at the end of Episode 28. No previews, later episodes, novel material, or endgame information.

Wei Rao catches Xiao’s eye in the crowd, and he urges everyone to drive the intruders out. Villagers armed with farm tools take on guards with swords, ready to die rather than see their Wengzhu taken. The Empress Dowager goes pale, and only Han Liao keeps her from being pulled out of her carriage. Wei Shu runs for a carriage to hide in, and finds Wei Rao already sitting inside.

The plague that wasn’t

It was all a plan. Wei Rao never had the plague. She soaked in ice water to make herself sick with a chill, so that Han Liao and Wei Shu would think it safe to move against her. The crown prince abandons the Dowager and his wife and rides off with a few guards. Wei Rao’s roadblocks steer him into the woods and into a pit she had dug in advance. Xiao makes a show of pulling him out, then has the pit covered over again and leaves him shouting in the forest.

Wei Rao leaves the north road open on purpose so Han Liao can get the Dowager away, and they run into Lu Zhuo riding back. Wei Rao rides up and tells her version first. Lu Zhuo sees through it and plays along. When she silently mouths an insult at the Dowager, the Dowager nearly chokes.

Lu Zhuo takes her home, but she will not tell him where the crown prince is. Xiao dodges the question too, a habit he has clearly picked up from her, before giving in and leading Lu Zhuo to the back hill. Lady Shou’an scolds Wei Rao for keeping her in the dark, and Wei Rao apologizes and explains what comes next.

The wager

When the Emperor returns, the Grand Commandant, Han Liao and the crown prince’s camp all call for Wei Rao’s execution. Lu Zhuo tells an anxious Liu Shuo not to plead for her; silence will help her more than any argument. Lady Li sends a doctor to the estate, and her attendant Ying Gu reports that the woman in Wei Rao’s bed is a maid. Wei Rao is nowhere to be found. She turns up instead in front of Wei Shu, who has been sent home to recover, and proposes a bet on whether the Emperor will have her killed. The stake is Wei Shu’s life.

The Emperor decides

In Xuanshi Hall, Liu Shuo asks only about his father’s health, which pleases the Emperor. Lu Zhuo takes on the whole court and wins by framing the crown prince’s conduct as a loss of the people’s support. The Emperor cannot accept a foolish heir, and that matters more to him than keeping the Lu family happy. Visiting the Dowager, he hears her insult Lady Shou’an, his former wet nurse, and she refuses to take it back. He walks out while she calls after him that she is his real mother. At Zhaoyang Hall he finds that Lady Shou’an has already gone to fetch his Baoyuan pills, and has left out the scallions he likes.

The decree goes against the crown prince’s side, and against Wei Shu, though she means to try one last time. At home, Wei Rao tells Lu Zhuo she has started to care how she looks to him. He takes it as a sign that she loves him. Qi Zhongkai tells him there is a simple way to find out.

Episode 28 is the payoff for every time Wei Rao kept a plan to herself. This time the secret is the plan, and it works on everyone, including the people on her side.
