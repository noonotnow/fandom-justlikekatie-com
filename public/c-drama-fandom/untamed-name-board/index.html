The Untamed / a pre-watch board

Three names to carry into a first watch.

A cultivation drama can hand you many names at once. In The Untamed, the opening cast of groups includes Gusu Lan, Yunmeng Jiang, and Lanling Jin. Here is a no-plot way to keep three labels apart:

Pre-watch / episode 0 · Names only · Original text and typography

01 / Place + family

Gusu Lan.

Gusu is the place-name part; Lan names the family. Don't confuse Lan with Lanling: similar English letters do not make these the same group.

02 / Place + family

Yunmeng Jiang.

Yunmeng is the place-name part; Jiang names the family. A real historical Yunmeng landscape exists, but this fictional group's home is not a documentary map of it.

03 / Place + family

Lanling Jin.

Lanling is the place-name part; Jin names the family. You can find Lanling in present-day Shandong, but that does not place the drama's fictional world on a present-day county map.

Keep the distinction

Place + family, not every person.

The repeatable trick is place + family, not “memorize every person.” Read the place-name note for real-world context, or choose your own safe-through episode in the Field Journal before loading its entries.
