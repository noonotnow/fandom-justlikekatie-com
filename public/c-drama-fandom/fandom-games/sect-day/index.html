Can you survive your first day in a sect?

Quiet Bell Sect welcomes beginners. Unfortunately, its mountain has not read the brochure.

Five decisions · Six possible endings · About 2–4 minutes

No skills. Considerable narrative responsibility.

Arrive as a visitor with no cultivation skills. An oath, a forbidden manual, and a sword with a staffing problem await. What you do early will matter later. Even leaving peacefully is a real ending.

No timer, account, or drama spoilers. This is an invented sect with invented characters, not a Legendary Grid.
