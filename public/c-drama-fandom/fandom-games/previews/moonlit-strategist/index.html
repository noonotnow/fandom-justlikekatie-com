Why these nine?

Xianxia turns personality into cosmology

Xianxia stories can make an inner conflict visible at the scale of realms, vows, heavenly law, spiritual power, and repeated lives. The archetypes in this Legendary Grid are not a taxonomy of every xianxia character. They are compact emotional doors: strategy, exile, chaos, healing, silence, cunning, duty, retreat, and fated return.

The game works because a choice is also an interpretation. You may read the Moonlit Strategist as control or care; the Bamboo Recluse as avoidance or clarity; the Fated Romantic as courage or repetition. The useful answer is the one that starts a conversation.
