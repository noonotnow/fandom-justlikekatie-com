How this guide was checked

The fan-maintained DramaWiki OST list helped us find the titles, but the release date, artist credits and legal links here come from the Apple Music album and track pages. The fan list describes 15 parts; Apple lists 48 album entries including additional recordings. We follow the platform's record for this pilot rather than treating the fan count as an album count.
